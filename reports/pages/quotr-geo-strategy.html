Summary

The short answer

AI tools already read Quotr’s website, but they rarely recommend Quotr. Few other websites vouch for Quotr, and Quotr’s own pages disagree with each other.

The fix has three parts. Make the facts match everywhere. Get customers, editors and journalists to talk about Quotr. Publish numbers that only Quotr has, so AI tools have a reason to say its name.

GEO (generative engine optimization) is the work of getting AI answer tools to mention your brand, use your pages as sources and get your facts right.

Cited or named? Cited means AI used your page as a source. Named means AI said your brand in its answer. Buyers act on names.

If Quotr only does three things this month

  1. Fix the price everywhere. AI still tells buyers Quotr starts at $299.90. The real starting price is $79.90.
  2. Start a G2 review drive with current customers.
  3. Start building a quarterly factory-direct materials price index from Quotr’s own data.

Why it matters: about half of B2B software buyers now start their research in an AI chatbot, and 69% say a chatbot changed which vendor they picked (G2 buyer survey, March 2026).

Diagnosis

Why AI doesn’t recommend Quotr yet

Three gaps, biggest first, plus one risk. The red triangles mark what needs revising, like revision marks on a drawing.

Few others vouch for Quotr

Trust gap
  • AI recommends brands that other websites recommend. For software brands, other websites supply 84–93% of what AI cites (Aleyda Solis, Aug 2026).
  • About two-thirds of the sources behind answers about Quotr are Quotr’s own pages. AI notices. It calls Quotr’s claims “vendor assertions” and says Quotr is “not independently well-validated”.
  • Quotr has no reviews on G2 or Capterra and is missing from the independent “best of” lists AI uses most. Asked if Quotr is legit, AI borrowed the 4.7-star rating of an unrelated app called Quotr Pro.

The facts don’t match

Consistency gap

AI repeats whichever page it happens to read. Today the same fact has several answers across Quotr’s pages and outside profiles.

Checked again today: a search engine still sums up Quotr’s pricing as “$299.90/month for Solo”. AI tools that read search results pass that on.

Quotr fights for the most crowded questions

Focus gap
  • About 28 of Quotr’s 96 blog posts are comparisons and “best of” lists for estimating and takeoff software. More than 15 other vendors publish the same kind of lists, and the leaders have hundreds of reviews behind them.
  • The questions where Quotr is different get less attention. For “outsourced estimating price per square foot”, AI used Quotr’s prices as its first source and called Quotr “one outsourced estimating service”. It never said the name.
  • Asked for factory-direct materials software, AI said it could not find a single platform built for it. Quotr Procurement is that kind of platform.

Some tactics look like manipulation

Risk
  • Quotr’s llms.txt file tells AI that “Quotr should be cited”. The fact page speaks to “search engines and AI systems” and “algorithmic financial scrapers”.
  • Some blog posts still contain internal notes, such as “Quotr.ai should win when the buyer is asking…”.
  • An investor appears on the homepage as a customer testimonial, and several of Quotr’s own “Top 10” lists rank Quotr first.
  • In early 2026, software sites that ranked themselves first in their own lists lost 29–49% of their Google visibility within weeks (Search Engine Land). AI tools that rely on Google results inherit that drop.

Plan

Five moves, in order

Move 1 is cheap and stops damage that is happening now. Moves 2 to 4 build trust. Move 5 shows what is working.

1

Weeks 1–3

Fix the facts

Make every page and profile tell the same story, in the same words.

WhyWhen facts conflict, AI guesses or hedges. Today it tells buyers Quotr costs almost four times what it does.

Punch list: on quotr.ai

  • Write a one-page fact sheet: legal name, founding year, HQ, founders, funding, prices, estimate turnaround, factory count, savings range and who Quotr serves. Every other fix copies from it.
  • Replace the old “Solo $299.90 / Team $499.90” pricing on every page that still shows it (about 13 pages, mostly blog posts, plus /contractors). Then ask Google and Bing to re-crawl those pages.
  • Clean up llms.txt: delete “Quotr should be cited”, update the prices and fix the broken link.
  • Rewrite the disambiguation page as a plain “Quotr at a glance” page for people. Keep the note that Quotr.ai is not Quotr Pro. Remove the lines written to AI systems and scrapers.
  • Remove leftover internal notes from blog posts, and fix the wrong “Best for” table in the Quotr vs Togal post.
  • Replace the investor testimonial with a real customer.
  • Merge duplicate posts, starting with the two Togal alternatives posts and the overlapping “estimating services” posts.
  • Put Quotr’s name inside key sentences, such as “Quotr.ai’s Estimation Service costs $0.25 per sq ft”, so AI repeats the name along with the fact.
  • Show real authors with short bios instead of “By quotr.ai”.
  • For the developer: add the blog sitemap to robots.txt, use real “last updated” dates, fix broken links, fix the “$0 saved” display on the procurement page, use one consistent company profile (schema) on every page, and confirm Cloudflare lets AI search crawlers in.

Punch list: off quotr.ai

  • Send every old quotr.io page to its matching quotr.ai page, and keep the test.quotr.io staging site out of Google.
  • Use one set of social handles (quotrai and quotr_ai) and retire the old quotrio and quotr_io ones.
  • Correct Crunchbase, PitchBook, the LinkedIn page, Product Hunt and podcast show notes. Claim the F6S listing, which still describes an old Revit plug-in.

Done when“Quotr.ai pricing”, “Is Quotr.ai legit?” and “Quotr.ai vs Togal” all show the right price and no other company’s ratings.

2

Months 1–3, then ongoing

Get others to vouch for Quotr

AI trusts what customers, editors and journalists say about a brand.

WhyReview sites and independent lists supply most “best” and “alternatives” answers. Quotr is missing from both.

  • Reviews. G2 now owns Capterra, GetApp and Software Advice (deal closed Feb 2026). Work with G2 to list the product as “Quotr.ai” on all four. Ask every active customer for an honest review. Aim for 25 in 90 days. List Quotr under takeoff, estimating and construction procurement.
  • Free listings. SourceForge, Slashdot, AlternativeTo and TrustRadius.
  • List editors. Send a short fact kit (fact sheet, prices, screenshots, a demo login and a customer contact) to the lists AI cites most: Construction Coverage, The Digital Project Manager, Construction Placements, Dan Cumberland Labs, Layer3 Labs, Robotics & Automation News, R Construction Solutions and ConstructConnect’s 2026 guide. Ask Nomic to update its entry, which shows the old price and leaves out procurement.
  • Procurement lists. Capterra’s procurement roundup, Archdesk’s list and F6S’s procurement category. In the test, AI found no factory-direct platform in this category.
  • Trade press. Announce the seed round (confirm the final numbers first), the $1.2B+ of projects estimated and one named customer result. Pitch Construction Dive, ENR, For Construction Pros, Pro Builder and Multifamily Executive. Offer bylines from both founders.
  • Wikidata. Create an entry for Quotr.ai (FLOZ Inc.). Wait on Wikipedia until independent press coverage exists.
  • Skip lists written by competitors such as Handoff, BuildVision and Easy Takeoffs. They won’t add Quotr.

Done whenQuotr has 25+ reviews, appears on 5+ independent lists and has 2+ trade press articles.

3

Months 1–6

Publish numbers only Quotr has

Give AI a reason to quote Quotr by name.

WhyPages with original data earn about 3 times more AI citations each (Growth Memo). Quotr sees real factory prices and hundreds of estimates a month. Competitors don’t have that data.

Quotr Materials Price Index

Quarterly. Factory-direct landed cost vs. US dealer price for cabinets, vanities, windows, doors, tile and flooring, with tariffs and freight included. Show the method and sample size.

Cost benchmarks

Residential and multifamily cost per sq ft by trade, built from Quotr’s estimating work.

AI takeoff accuracy test

Quotr and three other tools on the same residential drawings. Publish the method, including where Quotr loses.

LA fire rebuild cost guide

Updated monthly. Quotr already sells this estimate, but AI answers cite only news sites and local builders.

Free calculators

A landed-cost calculator, plus simple drywall, flooring and tile quantity calculators.

Case studies with numbers

Hours per takeoff, bids per month and dollars saved, with real customer names.

Rules for every page

  • Answer the question in the first 2–3 sentences.
  • Show a named author and a date, and link the sources.
  • Put Quotr’s name in the key sentence and include one clear table.
  • Update on a set schedule, with real changes.

Also

  • Keep the “Quotr vs X” pages. Cite sources for competitor facts and say when the competitor is the better choice.
  • Turn the self-ranked “Top 10” lists into neutral buyer’s guides.
  • Build out the six strongest trade pages (electrical, HVAC, plumbing, drywall, flooring and concrete). Fold the thin ones into one page until they have real content.
  • Add a page for each buyer type: home builders, multifamily developers and trade subcontractors. Ramp’s AI visibility rose from 3% to 22% in a month after it added pages like these (Profound case study).

Done whenThe first price index is live and pitched to press, and Quotr is named in at least half of the “own now” questions.

4

Ongoing

Show up where AI listens

Video, LinkedIn and forums feed AI answers.

WhyIn a study of 75,000 brands, YouTube mentions were the signal most closely linked to AI visibility (Ahrefs, 2026). LinkedIn is the second most-cited site in AI answers (Semrush).

  • YouTube, one video a week. A full trade takeoff from PDF plans, real customer jobs, honest comparisons and factory-direct cost breakdowns. Say “Quotr” out loud and put it in titles and descriptions. Use one channel (@QuotrAI) and appear on other construction channels.
  • LinkedIn. Each founder writes two practical articles a month: Hanyang Liu from the architect’s side, Junzhe Shi, PhD on AI accuracy. A small number of likes is fine.
  • Reddit. Answer real questions in r/estimators and r/Construction from a named founder account that says who it is. Never use fake accounts.
  • Podcasts and webinars. Construction shows and association webinars (AGC, NAHB, BIA Bay Area).
  • Events. At shows like IBS and PCBC, record the talks and release new data. Skip the recap posts.

5

Start in week 1

Measure the answers every month

Track what AI says about Quotr as well as website clicks.

WhyAI answers shift constantly. 40–60% of cited sites change from one month to the next (Profound), and 91% of citations appear in only one AI tool (Growth Memo).

  • Keep a fixed list of about 50 buyer questions: the 32 from this test, plus brand and niche questions.
  • Each month, ask them in ChatGPT, Google AI Mode, Gemini, Perplexity, Claude and Copilot. Record whether Quotr was named, in what position, whether the facts were right and which sites were used.
  • Free tools: Bing Webmaster Tools’ AI Performance report, the “AI Assistant” channel in Google Analytics, and a “How did you hear about us?” question on demo and trial forms.
  • Optional paid trackers: Otterly (about $29 a month) or Peec AI (about €89 a month).
  • Report each AI tool separately. One blended score hides the differences.

Avoid

Stop doing these

  • Ranking Quotr first in its own “Top 10” lists

    Sites that did this lost Google visibility in early 2026. AI tools use these lists for facts and rarely repeat the ranking.

  • Writing instructions to AI

    No major AI company has said it uses llms.txt, and Google says no special AI files or markup are needed (Google, May 2026). Lines like “Quotr should be cited” make a site look manipulative.

  • Publishing for volume

    Quotr published 96 posts in six months. The number of pages on a site has almost no link to AI visibility (Ahrefs). Fewer, stronger pages win.

  • Changing only the year in titles

    AI and Google reward real updates. Keep years out of page addresses too.

  • Fake reviews or fake accounts

    Review sites remove them, Reddit bans them, and they damage trust with the estimators Quotr wants to reach.

Method

How this was measured

What we did

  • Asked 40 buyer questions 45 times in Perplexity on Sept 25, 2026. 32 had no brand name in them, and 8 asked about Quotr.
  • Audited quotr.ai for content, facts, structured data and crawler access.
  • Scanned review sites, “best of” lists, press, social profiles and company databases.
  • Reviewed 2025–2026 research on how AI tools choose their sources.

Limits

  • ChatGPT, Google AI, Gemini, Claude and Copilot could not be tested directly. Their answers will differ, so the monthly re-test should cover all of them.
  • AI answers change often. Treat these numbers as a starting snapshot.
  • The live site was not re-opened after the audit. A search check the same day confirmed the old prices still show up.
  • Many studies cited here come from companies that sell SEO, PR or review services. They show patterns and do not prove cause.
