## Summary of reliability

**Overall:** the four notes are **reliable on what they observed directly on quotr.ai**.
- Every on-site count reproduced exactly: 96 blog posts, 55 dictionary terms, 23 trade pages, 6 tutorials, 4 case studies, and the monthly cadence.
- So did the robots.txt and sitemap findings, the llms.txt text, the schema findings, the leftover brief text, the AI Labyrinth link and the disambiguation contradictions.
- The headline visibility result also reproduced: Quotr is **absent from unbranded category answers**, and **quoted without its name** on pricing answers (C12; the how-to case, P5, could not be re-run). So did the branded-answer problems: stale $299.90 price, Quotr Pro app conflation, test.quotr.io citation, PitchBook funding conflict.

**Weaker areas:** claims taken from Perplexity summaries or third-party aggregators. I corrected eleven of them:

1. **The stale-pricing problem is bigger than reported, and directly observed.** ONSITE said the old "Solo/Team" tiers were "seen only through Perplexity" in 2 posts. The text is live on [/blog/stack-alternative/](https://quotr.ai/blog/stack-alternative/): "Solo from **$299.90/month**, Team (2–6 seats) $499.90/month, Enterprise (7+) custom". Search-index text shows the same line on at least 9 blog posts, and about 13 URLs in total carry an old price.
2. **HQ: San Francisco is on Quotr's own legal page.** [/terms/](https://quotr.ai/terms/) says "FLOZ INC., a Delaware corporation with offices at 495 27th Ave Unit 8, San Francisco, CA 94121". That is exactly the PitchBook address. The Berkeley/San Francisco conflict is therefore internal to quotr.ai, not just a blog-boilerplate or third-party error.
3. **/contractors/ is a canonicalized alias, not an unmanaged duplicate.** Its HTML carries `rel="canonical" href="https://quotr.ai/software"`. The real problem is that the search index still stores the *old* /contractors page, with its old title and Solo/Team pricing.
4. **PitchBook does not show "$190K seed, 15-Oct-2024".** VIS reported that figure. The live public [PitchBook profile](https://pitchbook.com/profiles/company/606944-17) shows one "Seed Round | 01-Jan-2025" with no amount (so OFFSITE is right). The $190K / Oct-2024 figure appears only inside Perplexity answers, and its origin is unverified.
5. **STACK's "1,398 reviews" are Capterra reviews, not G2.** OFFSITE's table listed the figure as G2. ConstructConnect's own quick-comparison table labels it "(1,398 reviews, Capterra)". Its per-product box mislabels it as G2.
6. **Nomic is not vendor-neutral, and it is not the only listicle naming Quotr.** Nomic ranks itself #1 ("Our pick") on its Kreo list. A second vendor listicle also names Quotr: [Octopus Builds, Sep 9, 2026](https://octopusbuilds.com/blog/ai-development-companies-ai-quoting-estimation), where Quotr is #2 of 8 with the stale Solo/Team pricing. ForesightIQ's Togal page names Quotr, but its only source is Quotr's own blog.
7. **"Quotr is listed first for the factory-direct query" did not hold up.** COMP reported #1. My re-run put Quotr **3rd of 6**, and the engine said Quotr "emphasize[s] sourcing and procurement more than AI takeoff".
8. **Factory count: there are more than two numbers.** Quotr publishes 220+, 50+ and "30+ audited manufacturers" (the last in indexed text). It also publishes "up to 50%", "40–50%" and "40–55%" savings claims.
9. **The /procurement/ "Client saved ~$0" bug needs a nuance.** It appears on all three cards in the "Completed projects" section. The featured Myren Dr card higher on the same page shows "~$91,800".
10. **Quotr's own comparison page has a competitor fact wrong.** [stack-alternative](https://quotr.ai/blog/stack-alternative/) calls PlanSwift "a Trimble product". PlanSwift is a ConstructConnect product, as shown in the [ConstructConnect guide](https://www.constructconnect.com/blog/ai-powered-takeoff-and-estimating-software-a-contractors-guide-to-the-top-players-in-2026). Perplexity already uses Quotr's PlanSwift posts as a fact source (VIS V3), so the error can spread into AI answers.
11. **Review directories: G2 now owns Capterra, GetApp and Software Advice.** G2 announced the acquisition from Gartner in January 2026, and it reportedly closed Feb 5, 2026. VIS's "Software Advice (Gartner network)" label is out of date. OFFSITE's "verify" is now confirmed.

**Unchanged and still unverified:**
- G2 review count for quotr-io (anti-bot wall)
- Reddit footprint
- ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, Gemini and Copilot answers
- The VIS results for V1, V3, C13 and P5 (the classifier refused the re-runs)
