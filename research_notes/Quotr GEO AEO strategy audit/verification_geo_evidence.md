**Corrections in this file override the original notes.** This file checks `geo_ai_citation_signals_2026.md` ("signals") and `geo_content_playbook_b2b.md` ("playbook"). If a claim is marked CORRECTED or UNVERIFIED here, use this file's wording in anything shown to Quotr.

## Summary of reliability

- **Overall: fair to good, with some specific errors.** Neither note's author could open primary pages, but most headline figures trace to real, correctly named studies, and both notes flag vendor bias well. Of 29 claims checked in detail:
  - 15 CONFIRMED (several with added nuance);
  - 13 CORRECTED, mostly on attribution, date, venue or context rather than the number itself;
  - 1 UNVERIFIED.

  About 15 more claims could not be re-checked (section D).
- **Most consequential corrections:**
  1. **Seer.** The "cited brands get +35% organic / +91% paid clicks" figure is **not** in the Seer 2026 update the notes cite. The 2026 update says cited = **+120% organic clicks per impression vs. not cited, but still −38% vs. queries with no AI Overview**. Seer itself says it "cannot claim causation".
  2. **Kaiser & Schulze** (ChatGPT traffic converts below every traditional channel except paid social) is a **peer-reviewed *Marketing Science* paper published online Apr 21, 2026**, not 2025. The paper adds that AI traffic performs better in **complex product categories**.
  3. **The GEO paper is not unreplicated.** C-SEO Bench (NeurIPS 2025) found most GEO-style content tricks "largely ineffective" and classic ranking "significantly more effective".
  4. **Attribution errors:**
     - the 68.5% / 66.75% / 66% format data is HubSpot's (June 2026), not AirOps';
     - the 30M-source "Reddit #1" study is Peec AI's, not Semrush's;
     - the Reddit 3.83% → 0.52% ChatGPT drop is Promptwatch data from **Aug 2026**, not a 2025 Semrush figure;
     - the 44.2% "first 30%" stat is from Indig's "The Science of How AI Pays Attention" (Feb 2026).
  5. **Aleyda Solis's Aug 2026 study** puts SaaS third-party share at **82.3%**, and most of it is **social/community/video (45.7%)**, not news/review (10.4%). This changes the playbook's off-site priority order: trade press and review sites matter less for SaaS than YouTube, Reddit and communities.
  6. **Arithmetic:** Quotr's Lite tier (~$959 per seat per year) is *below* the Capterra $1,600–$4,000 budget band, not inside it.
- **Biggest framing risks for the meeting:**
  - correlations presented as levers (YouTube mentions, first-30% placement, "cited → more clicks");
  - the playbook's "AI traffic converts far better".
- **Biggest gap: 2026 developments the notes missed.**
  - Google's June 3, 2026 **Search generative-AI opt-out control plus Search Console Generative AI performance reports**, global since Aug 31, 2026.
  - **Google I/O 2026:** AI Mode at 1B+ monthly users, AI Overviews at 2.5B, and AIO flowing into AI Mode.
  - **The first randomized field experiment on AI search clicks** (Aug 2026): AI Mode −18.8 pp external CTR; removing AIO +8.8 pp.
  - **OpenAI sponsored-agent ad tests** (Sept 2026).
- **What the corrections mean for the client's three questions:**
  1. **Content types:** prioritise original data and decision-support pages, honest comparisons and video. Comparison queries trigger AIOs 95% of the time, per Seer. Don't build a self-ranked listicle library.
  2. **Optimize vs create:** fix and consolidate what already ranks or is cited, but the evidence (Ahrefs' own caveat, Google's "non-commodity" test, C-SEO Bench) favours net-new, genuinely unique assets over frequent cosmetic refreshes.
  3. **Signals:** off-site brand presence (YouTube, communities, reviews, press) and classic organic retrievability are the best-supported signals. On-page "GEO tricks" and llms.txt/schema are weak or unsupported.
