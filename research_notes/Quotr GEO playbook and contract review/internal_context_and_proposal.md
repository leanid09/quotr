## Q1. What exactly does the sent proposal (Nov 13) commit to, deliverable by deliverable, and which commitments are risky?

### Takeaway
The sent PDF commits The Knowledge Company to 310 publishable assets in 90 days (186 website articles + 124 LinkedIn/X articles, 5 per publishing day), 13 weekly AI-visibility runs, 13 technical SEO checks, 13 GitHub updates, 3 monthly reports, 5-7 competitor monitoring and a read-only MCP MVP live by day 90, at $5,500/month ($16,500). It says "volumes and scope set in the agreement", but Quotr's draft agreement contains no volumes at all, so the PDF and the cover email are the only written anchor for what Leanid will be judged on. The 310-asset / 100-by-day-30 volume is the riskiest promise: it is roughly 1.7x Quotr's whole existing blog archive in 13 weeks, runs straight through the holidays, and conflicts with the spam-policy caution in Leanid's own earlier draft.

### Inferences
- Capacity math: the term (Nov 30 – Feb 28) has 65 weekdays, 61 after Christmas, New Year's, MLK Day and Presidents Day. "62 publishing days" therefore means publishing essentially every business day, including the Dec 21 – Jan 1 holiday stretch. Day 1-30 (Nov 30 – Dec 29) has ~21 business days, so "100 assets live" requires ~4.8 assets/business day from week 1, the same week the knowledge infrastructure, content audit, AI baseline and access setup are supposed to happen.
- 186 website articles in 13 weeks is ~14 per week. Quotr's best month ever was 25 posts (June), and its whole blog is 96-108 posts after ~6 months, so the plan more than doubles the blog in one quarter. With 3 posts/day on quotr.ai, every post also needs Quotr approval (PDF: "timely approvals"); approvals would fall on Jati/Mia/Jenny/Jaron, whose bandwidth is unknown.
- Spam/quality tension: Quotr's own team raised Google's spam policy on Sep 25 and is freezing listicles; Leanid's Nov 12 draft cited Google's scaled-content policy; yet the sent plan includes 62 competitor-focused articles at 1/day. The basis for the volume is Leanid's Nov 6 claim that Quotr's verified domain and backlinks allow 5-6 sitemap entries a day without being flagged (an opinion, not a measured limit). This is the most likely place for friction if quality slips.
- Items that depend on Quotr engineering or accounts and could slip outside Leanid's control: MCP MVP (Jaron said on Sep 25 they had not had time for MCP), GitHub updates (whose org/repo?), X articles (account access), LinkedIn articles (company page vs founder accounts), CMS publishing (Jaron keeps blog posts as MD files edited in Cursor), developer fixes (only 2 of 14 September fixes shipped in full per Leanid's Nov 12 audit).
- Monitoring of "Muse, Duet and Instinct" and Clarity/Bing was promised only in the email; there is no record of what these assistants are or how they would be measured. Treat as a promise to clarify or quietly re-scope at kickoff.
- Because the contract has no volumes, Leanid can propose writing the realistic deliverables and KPIs into Schedule A now (Tianyi invited comments on highlighted sections); otherwise "310 assets" and "100 by day 30" remain the de facto yardstick from the PDF.
- Cost exposure: Leanid told Tianyi on Nov 6 that a single article had grown from ~100K to ~2.5M tokens of model usage. At that rate 310 assets is on the order of 775M tokens, and the draft contract makes the Consultant bear all expenses.
