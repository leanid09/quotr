# A16. Add a human edit and fact-check step for AI-assisted drafts

- **What:** Before anything is published, one named person checks it against the fact sheet, removes any brief or prompt text, checks every number has a source and date, and checks competitor facts. Use a short pre-publish checklist.
- **Why (evidence):** Leftover internal notes, `utm_source=chatgpt.com` links and a wrong competitor fact suggest drafts went live without a final editing pass (report). Publishing AI drafts without editing is on the "stop doing" list ([[Content priorities]]).
- **Owner:** Marketing lead.
- **Effort / Impact:** S / M (prevents the same problems coming back).
- **Depends on:** nothing.
- **Done when:** the checklist is in use and every post published after Day 30 has a named editor.
- **How-to:** [[GEO writing style guide]] §7 (safe AI drafting workflow) and §8 (pre-publish checklist).
