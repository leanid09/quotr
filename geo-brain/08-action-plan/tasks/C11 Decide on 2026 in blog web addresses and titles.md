# C11. Decide on "2026" in blog web addresses and titles

- **What:** For each of the 27 posts with "2026" in the web address, and each post with "2026" in the title, pick one action and write it down:
  1. Already retired by a merge: nothing more to do.
  2. Move to a year-free address, with a 301 redirect, at a real update (every fact, price and entry re-checked).
  3. Keep it as a dated 2026 record, or give a real 2027 edition a year-free address.
  4. Leave it alone (event recaps, where the year is the subject).
  The post-by-post table is in [[Refresh plan Q4 2026#January 2027: the year in URLs and titles]].
- **Why (evidence):**
  - 27 post web addresses contain "2026". About 20 of them carry the year as a label, not as the subject ([[What went wrong]], W-16).
  - At least 10 live titles contain "2026", 5 of them on posts with no year in the web address. Most live titles were never recorded, so check each page.
  - Practitioners reported that posts "lightly refreshed with '2026' in the title" were among the pages that lost Google visibility in early 2026. Google has not confirmed this (FRESH-08, QUALITY-11).
  - Our Google claim set has no official guidance on year-stamped web addresses at year end ([[Google search updates 2025-2026]]).
  - [[Content refresh playbook]] (rule 6) says to decide post by post by mid-December 2026, and that no task covered this yet.
- **Owner:** GEO consultant prepares the list and the Search Console numbers. Marketing agrees each decision. Dev adds the 301s in January.
- **Effort / Impact:** S (the decision) / M. The address moves themselves are part of each post's refresh.
- **Depends on:** Search Console data for each address (A17), and knowing which posts got a real refresh in Q4 (A18).
- **Done when:** every post with "2026" in its web address or title has a written decision in its article note Log by 2026-12-18; the January moves are live by 2027-01-15, each in one hop with a 301, links and sitemap updated; no post was bulk-renamed to 2027.
- **How-to:**
  - Decide in the week of 2026-12-14. Move addresses in the weeks of 2027-01-04 and 2027-01-11, and only if no Google update is rolling out.
  - Point older redirects straight at the final address, so there is never a chain of two redirects.
  - Titles: keep "2026" only while every entry is checked for 2026. Never swap "2026" for "2027" without a real update.
  - Guides: [[Refresh plan Q4 2026]]; [[Content refresh playbook]] (rule 6, "Years in titles and web addresses"); [[GEO writing style guide]].
