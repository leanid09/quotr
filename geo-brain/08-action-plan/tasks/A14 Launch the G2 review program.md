# A14. Launch the G2 review program

- **What:** Claim or update the G2 profile; create the Capterra listing (G2 now owns Capterra, GetApp and Software Advice, so check whether one onboarding covers all four). Build a list of every active customer. Ask all of them at the same milestones (not just the happy ones). Use G2-run campaigns if offering any incentive. Reply to every review. Aim for **10–30 honest reviews** (report).
- **Why (evidence):** Capterra and G2 each fed 9 of the 32 unbranded AI answers. List writers such as ConstructConnect describe tools largely through their G2 and Capterra ratings. Competitors show proof: Togal.AI 4.8 stars from 60 G2 reviews, Beam AI 4.9 from 30, STACK about 1,400 Capterra reviews. Quotr has no Capterra listing, and Perplexity reports zero G2 reviews (G2 blocked our tools, so that count is unverified). Without real reviews, AI borrows the unrelated Quotr Pro app's "37 ratings and a 4.7/5 score" when asked "Is Quotr.ai legit?" (report; verification file, claims 30–32).
- **Owner:** Customer success (TO CONFIRM who); CEO sponsors and sends the first asks personally.
- **Effort / Impact:** L (ongoing) / H.
- **Depends on:** the customer list; G2 profile access. Rules first: the FTC's rule on reviews and testimonials (in force since October 21, 2024) bans fake reviews, sentiment-based incentives, review suppression and undisclosed insider reviews; G2 bans "gating" (only asking happy customers).
- **Done when (day 30):** profiles claimed; ask list built; first wave of asks sent; process written down; first reviews live. Suggested 90-day target: 10 or more genuine reviews ([[KPIs and dashboard]], KPI L7). Target the **count**, never the rating.
