# B7. Founder presence: LinkedIn and estimator communities

- **What:** Each founder writes one LinkedIn article a month (topics: residential AI takeoff accuracy, factory-direct buying, tariffs). The CTO and an in-house estimator post 2–3 genuinely helpful answers a week in r/estimators (for example the thread ["Is there an AI automated takeoff software tool?"](https://www.reddit.com/r/estimators/comments/1qjzjfi/is_there_an_ai_automated_takeoff_software_tool/)) and in Facebook estimator groups, always disclosing "I work at Quotr".
- **Why (evidence):** Social, community and video sites are the largest outside source type for SaaS in AI answers, and estimators there are asking whether AI takeoff works (report). No Reddit thread naming Quotr was found. Do not bet on one platform: Reddit's share of ChatGPT citations fell from 3.83% to 0.52% within weeks in August 2026 (report, citing Promptwatch).
