## How to read this plan

**A few terms, in plain English**
- **GEO / AEO** (generative engine optimization / answer engine optimization): getting a brand found, named and linked inside AI answers such as ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews and AI Mode, Perplexity, Gemini, Claude and Copilot.
- **Named** = the AI says "Quotr" in its answer. **Cited** = the AI uses a quotr.ai page as a source footnote. The two are different: today AI often cites Quotr's pages without naming Quotr.
- **Unbranded question** = a buyer question that does not mention Quotr ("best AI takeoff software for subcontractors").
- **Re-crawl** = asking Google and Bing to read a page again after you fix it. **301 redirect** = a permanent forward from an old web address to a new one. **noindex** = a tag that tells search engines not to show a page.
- **Sitemap** = a list of a site's pages for search engines. **lastmod** = the "last changed" date in that list.
- **Schema** = hidden labels in a page's code that describe facts (company name, price, author) to machines. **llms.txt** = a text file some sites add as a guide for AI tools.
- **Staging site** = a test copy of the website (Quotr's is test.quotr.io). It should never appear in search.

**Timing.** Day 1 is the first working day after the kickoff meeting. This page assumes a start in early October 2026, so:
- **Days 1–30 ≈ October 2026** (quick wins and clean-up)
- **Days 31–60 ≈ November 2026** (build the proof engine)
- **Days 61–90 ≈ December 2026** (publish what only Quotr can publish; day-90 review in late December)
- **6-month horizon ≈ January–March 2027** (scale what works; 6-month review in late March 2027)

If the kickoff moves, shift the dates, not the order.

**Effort**
- **S** = up to 2 working days in total.
- **M** = 3–10 working days, or a few hours a week for a month.
- **L** = more than 2 weeks of work, or an ongoing program.

**Impact**
- **H** = fixes an error AI is repeating today, or directly moves a tracked AI-answer number (named rate, branded accuracy, reviews, list inclusion).
- **M** = clear support for an H task, or a likely but slower gain.
- **L** = housekeeping. Cheap and sensible, but not a proven lever.

**Evidence caveat.** All AI-answer tests so far used **one engine: Perplexity (Sonar model), on 2026-09-25.** ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews / AI Mode, Gemini, Claude and Copilot were not tested. That is why a multi-engine baseline (task A15) is in the first two weeks ([[AI visibility baseline]]).

## Stop doing (or pause)

| Stop or pause | Why | Evidence and where to read more |
|---|---|---|
| **Adding more self-ranked "best X" lists** (Quotr ranked #1 on its own site) | Perplexity did not accept the self-ranking in our tests; B2B sites that did this lost 29–49% of Google visibility in early 2026; the author is left out of the AI Overview recommendation 69% of the time | Report; [[Myths and risks]] §7 |
| **More "[trade] estimating services" and city-by-city variations** | About 12 services posts already overlap; Google warns against separate pages for every variation of a question | Report; [[Content priorities]] |
| **Volume targets** (the 22–25 posts a month pace of May–June 2026) | A 75,000-brand Ahrefs study found almost no link between page count and AI visibility; aim for about 8–12 better pieces a month | Report; [[Content priorities]] ("Recommended mix") |
| **"Refreshing" by changing only the year or date** | Posts "lightly refreshed with '2026' in the title" were among the pages that lost Google visibility; update when facts change and say what changed | Report; [[Page refresh checklist]] |
| **Publishing AI drafts without a human edit** | Leftover internal notes and tracking tags went live | Report; task A16 |
| **Instructions or wording aimed at AI and bots** ("Quotr should be cited", "For search engines and AI systems…", "algorithmic financial scrapers") | Reads as an attempt to steer AI; resembles the prompt injections researchers flag in llms.txt files | Report; [[Myths and risks]] §4–5 |
| **"By quotr.ai" bylines** | A company is not an expert author | Task A7 |
| **Presenting investors as customers** | Label connections clearly; use real customer quotes | Task A6 |
| **Unsourced statistics and headline claims without a method** ($1.2B+, 300+ projects a month, 95–99% accuracy, "$177 billion a year") | AI engines flag them as self-published; buyers can't check them | [[Positioning and proof points]] §6 |
| **Calling repackaged public numbers a "report"** | Not unique; add Quotr data or don't call it original research | [[Content priorities]] |
| **Expanding schema or llms.txt as a lever** | Google says no special files or markup are needed; fix conflicts, don't expand | Report; [[Schema markup kit]] §2 |
| **Any review shortcut** (fake, paid-for-positive, gated or employee reviews) | Illegal under the FTC's reviews rule and against G2 and Capterra rules | [[Review generation]] §6 |
| **Wikipedia editing now, or any "Wikipedia hack"** | An article is unlikely to stick without independent press; undisclosed editing breaks Wikipedia's rules | Report; [[Wikidata and knowledge graph]] §6 |
| **Opting out of Google's AI features** | Quotr needs visibility there | Report; [[Tracking setup]] §3.3 |
| **Year-stamped URLs for new pages** | "2026" slugs will look stale in 2027; keep years in titles only when the content is truly updated | Report; [[GEO tactics already used]] (tactic 21) |

## Risks that could slow the plan

| Risk | Early warning sign | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| Fact decisions take weeks | A1 not signed off by the end of week 1 | Fix pricing (already confirmed) and llms.txt anyway; mark other facts TO CONFIRM and leave them out of copy until decided |
| No one owns reviews | No asks sent by day 30 | CEO sends the first 10 asks personally; assign CS owner at the next check-in |
| Data can't be published | B5 blocked by customer or supplier concerns | Aggregate more (ranges, medians, no supplier names); start with the LA rebuild guide or turnaround data instead |
| Developer time is scarce | A9–A11 slip past week 2 | Do the one-day technical batch first; delay calculators, not hygiene |
| Results look flat after one month | Named rate unchanged in November | Expected: reviews, lists and press take months. Judge the branded-answer fixes first (price, staging, factory count) |
| Engines change behavior suddenly | Big swings between monthly runs | Test across six engines (ChatGPT, Google AI Mode/AI Overviews, Perplexity and Gemini monthly; Claude and Copilot quarterly, per [[Tracking set]]); count a change only if it holds two months in a row ([[KPIs and dashboard]] §5) |
| Capacity squeezed by a return to volume publishing | Output climbs back toward 20+ posts a month | Hold the quality bar; drop P3 roadmap items first |
