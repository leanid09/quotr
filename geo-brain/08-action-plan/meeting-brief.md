## How to use this brief

- **Tone:** warm, specific and collaborative. Quotr's team has done a lot of deliberate GEO work, fast. The message is "you built a strong engine; now let's make sure AI credits it to you," not "you did it wrong."
- **Words to use the first time, in plain English:**
  - **GEO / AEO** (generative engine optimization / answer engine optimization): getting a brand named and linked inside AI answers such as ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews and AI Mode, Perplexity, Gemini, Claude and Copilot.
  - **Named** = the AI says "Quotr" in its answer. **Cited** = the AI uses a quotr.ai page as a source footnote.
  - **Unbranded question** = a buyer question that does not mention Quotr, like "best AI takeoff software for subcontractors 2026".
  - **Top of funnel** = people at the early research stage, not yet shopping for a product.
- **One caveat to say early and often:** the AI tests used **one engine, Perplexity** (its developer version, Sonar), on September 25, 2026. ChatGPT and Google's AI answers still need a proper baseline. The first job after this meeting is that baseline.
- **Bring:** the [presence scorecard](../02-current-state/presence-scorecard.md), the fact sheet's inconsistency register ([entity-fact-sheet.md](../00-quotr/entity-fact-sheet.md) §4), the [30-60-90 plan](30-60-90-plan.md), and three short screenshots or quotes: the outsourced-estimating answer ("some firms"), the "Quotr.ai vs Togal.AI" answer ("from about $299.90/month") and the "Is Quotr.ai legit?" answer (Quotr Pro's ratings).

## 1. The situation in one paragraph

In about six months, Quotr's team has built one of the most deliberate GEO programs in construction tech: 96 blog posts, 55 glossary terms, 23 trade pages, answer-first formatting, a "we are not Quotation" entity page and an llms.txt file. AI engines do read it. A quotr.ai page appeared in the sources of 6 of 32 unbranded buyer questions we asked Perplexity, as often as reddit.com. But Quotr was **named in only 1 of those 32 answers**, while STACK, PlanSwift and Buildxact were each named in 10. When buyers ask about Quotr by name, the answers are mostly right, but they repeat errors that start on Quotr's own pages: a starting price nearly four times too high, star ratings borrowed from an unrelated "Quotr Pro" app, and conflicting funding figures. So the gap is not content volume. It is **consistent facts, and proof on other people's websites**. The good news is that the first fixes cost days, not months. And the topics where almost no software company appears in AI answers (factory-direct prices, tariffs and landed costs, multifamily and rebuild costs) are exactly where Quotr holds data no rival has.

---

## 2. Three things we want the team to leave with

1. **"AI trusts your facts but not your recommendation."** Your content already feeds AI answers. The missing layer is other people vouching for Quotr.
2. **"One fact sheet first."** Nothing else works until price, HQ, funding, factory count and turnaround say the same thing everywhere. It's a 1–2 day decision for leadership.
3. **"Publish what only Quotr can know."** Your pricing and project data is the biggest content opportunity, and a strong top-of-funnel play.

## 3. Answers to the team's three questions

### Question 1: "What types of content should we prioritize for GEO/AEO?"

**Short answer:** Content an AI cannot piece together from anywhere else. In order: original data, free tools, a few honest comparison pages, video, and proof pages.

**Why we're confident:** Google's own May 2026 guide to AI search says its main advice is to publish "valuable, unique, non-commodity content", and that no special files, markup or "chunking" are needed ([Google](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/ai-optimization-guide)). A peer-reviewed 2025 benchmark (C-SEO Bench) found most popular GEO content tricks "largely ineffective" and classic SEO "significantly more effective" ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.11097)).

| Priority | Content type | Single strongest proof point | What it looks like for Quotr |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Original data and benchmarks** | In a Growth Memo study of 1,075 AI citations, primary-research pages earned **3.3 times** more citations per page ([Growth Memo](https://www.growth-memo.com/p/why-most-original-data-never-gets)). Our tariff question cited only government reports, trade groups, news media and large industry firms; **no software vendor** | A quarterly "factory-direct vs US price" index (cabinets, tile, windows, flooring, with tariffs and shipping); a residential and multifamily cost-per-sq-ft dataset from Estimation Service jobs; an LA fire-rebuild cost guide |
| 2 | **Free tools: calculators and templates** | For "how to estimate drywall for a house", most sources AI used were calculators, including free ones from rivals EasyTakeoffs and Procore | Trade material calculators; a landed-cost calculator (the total cost of imported goods, including shipping and duties); estimate and bid templates |
| 3 | **A few honest comparison pages** | "X vs Y" searches trigger a Google AI Overview 95% of the time ([Seer](https://www.seerinteractive.com/insights/aio-impact-on-google-ctr-2026-update)). But when a company's own "best of" list was cited in an AI Overview, the company itself was left out of the recommendation 69% of the time ([Lily Ray](https://lilyraynyc.substack.com/p/why-calling-yourself-the-best-could)) | Keep 5–8 head-to-head pages. Fact-check and date them, and say where each rival is stronger |
| 4 | **Video** | Aleyda Solis's August 2026 study found YouTube to be the largest single source site for SaaS brands in AI answers ([Aleyda Solis](https://www.aleydasolis.com/en/ai-search/ai-search-citations/)). (Perplexity never cited YouTube in our tests, so the payoff is likelier in Google and ChatGPT) | One trade per video on real residential plans; customer jobs; "Quotr.ai" said out loud and in titles |
| 5 | **Proof pages** | Perplexity currently calls Quotr's claims "vendor assertions" | Case studies with numbers and named customers; an integrations page; a security page; a public accuracy test |

**Do less of:** more self-ranked "best X" lists, more "[trade] estimating services" or city-by-city variations, and short generic definitions. Google warns that pages made for every variation of a question, mainly to game search or AI answers, can break its spam rules.

### Question 2: "How should we think about optimizing existing content versus creating new content?"

**Short answer:** Fix first, because some old pages are actively hurting Quotr. Then put most new effort into unique assets, not constant refreshing.

**The sequence:**
1. **Fix (weeks 1–2).** Correct the old pricing on about 13 URLs and llms.txt, and every other conflicting fact. Ask Google and Bing to re-read the pages. Ask Nomic and Octopus Builds to update their lists. This is the fastest win in the audit.
2. **Merge and attach the brand (weeks 2–6).** Merge the two Togal posts and the overlapping "estimating services" posts. Rewrite key sentences so the name travels with the fact: "Quotr.ai's Estimation Service charges $0.25 per sq ft for projects under 50,000 sq ft." Start with pages AI already fetches.
3. **Build new (from month 2).** Most new effort goes into data, tools and proof.

**Why:**
- Freshness matters less than many GEO guides claim. Ahrefs found AI-cited pages are fresher than ordinary search results, especially in ChatGPT, but they still average **2.9 years old**. The study's author suspects most brands will "see better results from creating new, high-quality content than from … extremely frequent content updating" ([Ahrefs](https://ahrefs.com/blog/do-ai-assistants-prefer-to-cite-fresh-content/)).
- Changing a date without changing the content can backfire: posts "lightly refreshed with '2026' in the title" were among the pages that lost Google visibility in early 2026 ([Lily Ray](https://lilyraynyc.substack.com/p/is-google-finally-cracking-down-on)).

**One rule for the team:** every new page must contain something no competitor or AI could write without Quotr's data, customers or people.

**Suggested effort split** (a planning guide, not a measured optimum): October is about 75% fixing and refreshing. From November to March, about 20–25% refresh, 45–50% new unique assets, and 20–25% support for off-site work.

### Question 3: "What signals or factors are becoming increasingly important for AI models when surfacing or citing content?"

**Short answer:** Proof from other people (reviews, independent lists, YouTube, communities, press), consistent facts everywhere, specific dated numbers, and plain good SEO. On-page tricks, llms.txt and special schema matter little.

| Signal | Direction | Single strongest proof point | What it means for Quotr |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Mentions and reviews on other sites** | Strong, and the most important | For SaaS brands, **82.3%** of the top cited sources in AI Mode, Gemini and ChatGPT were other people's sites; most were social, community and video sites (45.7%), not news or review sites (10.4%) ([Aleyda Solis](https://www.aleydasolis.com/en/ai-search/ai-search-citations/)) | Reviews, independent lists, YouTube, Reddit, Facebook estimator groups, LinkedIn |
| **Brand mentions more than links** | Strong correlation (not proven cause) | In Ahrefs' 75,000-brand study, YouTube mentions and brand mentions tracked AI visibility more closely than link-based authority ([Ahrefs](https://ahrefs.com/blog/ai-brand-visibility-correlations/)) | Get Quotr *talked about*, not just linked to |
| **Normal search visibility** | Strong; still the foundation | 38% of pages cited in Google's AI Overviews ranked top 10 for the same search in 2026, so a top-10 ranking helps but is not required ([Ahrefs](https://ahrefs.com/blog/ai-overview-citations-top-10/)) | Good SEO still feeds AI. Bing matters too, because it powers Copilot |
| **Unique, specific facts (numbers, dates)** | Rising | Growth Memo found dates and numbers are the details that best predict ChatGPT citations ([Growth Memo](https://www.growth-memo.com/p/why-proprietary-data-is-your-most)) | Publish dated figures from Quotr's own work |
| **Being named, not just footnoted** | Rising | Since May 7, 2026, ChatGPT links brand names straight to their homepages. Profound, a vendor, says B2B software gained most, with daily ChatGPT referrals up more than 200% ([Profound](https://www.tryprofound.com/blog/chatgpt-referrals-branded-links)). OpenAI can change this at any time | Each mention of "Quotr" is now a clickable doorway; the homepage must convert first-time visitors |
| **Consistent facts everywhere** | Rising (our inference, supported by the tests) | Perplexity hedged on Quotr's factory count and funding because sources disagreed | One fact sheet, used on every page and profile |
| **Real freshness** | Moderate; varies by engine | ChatGPT prefers newer pages; Google's AI Overviews care much less ([Ahrefs](https://ahrefs.com/blog/do-ai-assistants-prefer-to-cite-fresh-content/)) | Refresh pricing and comparison pages when facts change |
| llms.txt, special schema for AI, GEO "tricks" (packing in stats and quotes) | Weak | Google treats llms.txt like any other file and says no special markup is needed; C-SEO Bench found the tricks largely ineffective | Keep llms.txt short and accurate; fix schema conflicts; use real data because it helps readers |
| Betting on one platform | Risky | Reddit's share of ChatGPT citations fell from 3.83% to 0.52% within weeks in August 2026 ([Promptwatch](https://promptwatch.com/blog/chatgpt-stop-citing-reddit)) | Spread presence across channels; re-test monthly |

**One-line summary to say in the room:** "GEO is mostly good SEO, plus getting other people to talk about you." (Search Engine Journal summed up Google's guide as calling AEO and GEO "still SEO" ([SEJ](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/googles-new-ai-search-guide-calls-aeo-and-geo-still-seo/575026/)).)

### The follow-up question: "How do we maintain and expand top-of-funnel reach?"

**Short answer:** Measure reach as **presence in AI answers**, not only as website visits. Then grow it three ways: be named on the topics no one owns, build assets people still click through to, and be present where AI reads beyond quotr.ai.

**What's happening to clicks** (be honest about it):
- The first randomized experiment on this (a preprint, August 2026, about 1,100 Google users) found that moving people to Google's AI Mode cut their clicks to outside websites by **18.8 percentage points**. Removing AI Overviews *raised* clicks by 8.8 points ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.18352v1)).
- SparkToro reports **68%** of US Google searches ended without a click in early 2026 ([SparkToro](https://sparktoro.com/blog/in-2026-less-than-one-third-of-google-searches-still-send-a-click/)).
- Google says AI Mode passed **1 billion** monthly users and AI Overviews **2.5 billion** in May 2026 ([Google](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/products/search/search-io-2026/)).
- Being cited helps but does not undo the loss: pages cited in an AI Overview got 120% more clicks per view than uncited pages, but still 38% fewer than on searches with no AI Overview (Seer; a correlation, not proof).
- Whether AI visitors convert is unproven for B2B. The only peer-reviewed study (973 online stores) found ChatGPT visitors bought less often than every traditional channel except paid social, but did better for complex products ([Marketing Science](https://pubsonline.informs.org/doi/10.1287/mksc.2025.0489)). Quotr should measure this, not assume it.

**Three ways to grow reach:**
1. **Be named on the unclaimed topics:** factory-direct buying, tariffs and landed costs, multifamily cost benchmarks, residential rebuild costs.
2. **Build assets people still click through to:** tools, datasets, videos.
3. **Be present where AI reads beyond quotr.ai:** review sites, independent lists, YouTube, communities.

**Six-number top-of-funnel scorecard (from the report):** share of AI answers that name Quotr; AI impressions in Search Console; branded search volume; review count; YouTube views; demos that came from AI.

**Free tools that make it measurable:** Google Search Console's AI report (AI Overviews and AI Mode impressions by page, worldwide since August 31, 2026; no clicks); Bing Webmaster Tools' AI Performance report (Copilot citations, preview); GA4's "AI Assistant" channel (added May 2026; it misses Perplexity, so add a custom rule); a "How did you hear about us?" field on demo and trial forms; and a monthly test of about 50 buyer questions across six AI tools. Also: Google now lets sites opt out of its AI features; Quotr should **not** use it.

## 5. Lead with what's working

Say these first. They are true, and they set the tone.

- **Speed and intent.** A large library in about six months, peaking at 22–25 posts a month in May and June 2026. The CEO's March 2026 podcast even listed "GEO Strategy … focusing on 'Unbranded' search terms" as a topic. That is exactly the right goal; the tests confirm it's where the gap is.
- **The format is right.** "Quick answer" boxes, question headings, tables, FAQs and visible dates. Keep it.
- **The entity page works.** "What is Quotr?" is answered accurately.
- **Transparent pricing.** When AI reads /pricing/, it gets the price right. Few rivals publish per-sq-ft service prices at all.
- **Early mover.** Quotr has an llms.txt file; the research found no evidence that the six main competitors (Togal, Beam AI, Kreo, STACK, Handoff, Buildxact) publish one.
- **Real assets to build on:** named customers (RL Electric, AlphaX, BiltWise Structures, Salisbury Moore), real procurement project prices (for example a Saratoga job at $97,000 against a $187K–$218K local market price), a sample deliverables library, an ROI calculator and expert founders.

## 6. Raising the harder findings with care

A few findings could feel awkward. None of them is serious on its own, and all are quick to fix. Together, the report says, "they can signal to AI systems and skeptical estimators alike that the claims are not being checked."

**Principles for these conversations**
1. **Credit first, then the fix.** Open each item with what the team got right.
2. **Talk about systems, not people.** "An editing step is missing," never "someone made a mistake."
3. **Pair every issue with a fix and a time estimate.** Most are minutes to a day.
4. **Show, don't read out.** Put the exact lines on a slide or in a follow-up doc rather than quoting them aloud in a way that sounds like mockery.
5. **Ask, don't assert, on anything involving investors or funding.** We don't know why numbers differ; Quotr does.
6. **Use neutral words.** Say "can read as an attempt to steer AI," not "manipulation," "spam" or "fake."
7. **Offer to share the detailed list privately** with the marketing lead first, if they prefer.

## 7. Questions to ask Quotr

If time is short, ask the six marked ★.

| # | Topic | Question | Why we ask |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 ★ | Facts | Which numbers are final: price wording, HQ, founding year, founders and titles, funding and investors, factory count, savings range, turnaround? Who signs off, and by when? | Nothing else works until these match everywhere |

## 9. Questions the team may ask, with short answers

| They may ask | Short answer |
|---|---|
| "We already did GEO. Why aren't we showing up?" | The on-site part is largely done well: AI finds and reads Quotr. What's missing is the part AI now weighs most: consistent facts, and other people vouching for Quotr. |
| "Should we stop blogging?" | No. Slow the pace and raise the bar: about 8–12 better pieces a month instead of 22–25, each with something only Quotr can say. A 75,000-brand Ahrefs study found almost no link between page count and AI visibility. |
| "Is llms.txt a waste of time?" | Low value, low cost. Keep it short and accurate, or drop it. Just remove the "should be cited" line and the old prices. |
| "Can we pay to appear in ChatGPT?" | ChatGPT ads sit next to answers, not inside them; OpenAI says ads don't change answers. It began testing advertiser-sponsored agents in September 2026. Worth watching, and testing only if the costs make sense. |
| "Is one engine representative?" | It's a strong signal, not the full picture. Retrieval looked stable across re-runs, but brand order moved. That's why the first task is a baseline across ChatGPT, Google AI Mode / AI Overviews, Gemini, Perplexity, Claude and Copilot. |
| "How fast will we see results?" | Branded fixes first: once engines re-read the fixed pages, answers should quote $79.90 (we'll check about four weeks after the fixes). Reviews, lists and press take months. The borrowed Quotr Pro ratings fade only as real Quotr reviews appear. |
| "Should we get a Wikipedia page?" | Not yet. Wikipedia requires significant independent coverage, which Quotr doesn't have yet. Start with a Wikidata entry and earn press first. |
| "Does AI traffic even convert?" | Unproven for B2B. The one peer-reviewed study (online stores) found ChatGPT visitors converted below most channels, but better for complex products. We'll measure it in Quotr's own GA4 and CRM. |
