## What this is, in plain words

This folder holds everything we know about making **Quotr.ai** show up in AI answers: when someone asks ChatGPT, Google's AI Overviews or AI Mode, Perplexity, Gemini, Claude or Copilot about construction takeoff, estimating, bidding or buying materials. That work is called **GEO** (generative engine optimization) or **AEO** (answer engine optimization).

Quotr.ai (formerly Quotr.io) is AI construction takeoff, estimating and bid software from FLOZ Inc., with a done-for-you estimating service and factory-direct material procurement.

The brain has 68 Markdown pages: three at the top level (this page, the AI context pack and the source list), 63 in nine numbered topic folders, and two in `_meta` (QA log and changelog). It covers who Quotr is, how AI search works, where Quotr stands today, competitors, the questions buyers ask, what to publish, step-by-step playbooks and templates, how to measure progress, and a 90-day plan. It was built from a September 2026 research audit and two independent fact-checks (see [[Sources]]).

**Who it is for:**
- **The GEO consultant** advising Quotr (meeting prep, planning, monthly reviews).
- **Quotr's marketing team** (what to fix, what to write, what to measure).
- **Writers and developers** doing the work (rules, templates, technical fixes).
- **AI tools** used by any of the above: the pages are written so they can be pasted or uploaded as context.

**Three rules for everyone:**
1. **Facts about Quotr come from one page:** [[Entity fact sheet]]. If a fact is marked **TO CONFIRM with Quotr**, do not publish it.
2. **Only Perplexity was tested** (on 2026-09-25). ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews and AI Mode, Gemini, Claude and Copilot have no baseline yet. Don't claim results for them.
3. **Where sources disagree, the fact-checks win**, then the report, then the raw notes (see [[Sources]]).

## The 10 things to know in 60 seconds

1. **Only one engine was tested.** All the AI results come from Perplexity (its developer version, Sonar) on September 25, 2026. The first job is a baseline in ChatGPT and Google.
2. **Quotr is nearly invisible in unbranded questions.** Across 32 buyer questions that did not name Quotr, it was **named in 1 (about 3%)**. **STACK, PlanSwift and Buildxact were named in 10 each**, and Togal.AI in 8.
3. **AI reads Quotr, then drops the name.** A quotr.ai page appeared in the **source list of 6 of 32 answers**, as often as reddit.com. For outsourced estimating prices, Perplexity used Quotr's $0.25/$0.10 per sq ft rates but credited "some firms".
4. **Branded answers repeat Quotr's own old facts.** AI quotes a starting price of "$299.90", **nearly four times** the real **Lite price of $79.90** (Plus is $299.90 per seat per month). The retired "Solo/Team" pricing is still on **about 13 Quotr URLs** (mostly blog posts, plus the old indexed /contractors copy) and in llms.txt. AI also borrows an unrelated "Quotr Pro" app's 37 ratings and 4.7 score, and cites a "$190K seed" that PitchBook does not show.
5. **Quotr's basic facts change from page to page.** HQ (San Francisco or Berkeley), founding year (2023 or 2024), funding ($3.5M or "$5 million"), factory count (220+, 50+ or 30+ audited), savings and turnaround all conflict. A signed-off fact sheet comes before everything else.
6. **There is almost no proof on other people's websites.** Only three outside pages are confirmed to name Quotr: Nomic, Octopus Builds and ForesightIQ. None is independent. G2 reportedly shows about 0 reviews (unverified), there is no Capterra listing, and no earned trade press was found. Meanwhile, review sites fed many answers (Capterra and G2 each fed 9 of 32).
7. **One review program now reaches four sites.** G2 agreed to buy Capterra, GetApp and Software Advice from Gartner (announced January 2026), so one G2 review drive feeds all four.
8. **The team built a lot, fast.** In about six months it built **96 blog posts, 55 glossary terms and 23 trade pages**, with good answer-first formatting. The weak spots: self-ranked "best of" lists, thin trade pages, leftover internal notes in some posts, and an llms.txt line telling AI that "Quotr should be cited".
9. **GEO is mostly good SEO plus other people talking about you.** Google's May 2026 guide says no special files or markup are needed and asks for "non-commodity content". A peer-reviewed benchmark found most GEO content tricks "largely ineffective". For SaaS brands, 82.3% of top-cited sources were other people's sites.
10. **The biggest opening matches Quotr's own data.** On factory-direct prices, tariffs and landed costs, and multifamily and rebuild cost benchmarks, almost no software vendor is cited. Quotr has project data here (for example, a Saratoga job at $97,000 against a $187K–$218K local price). The first 30 days are about clean-up: one fact sheet, the price fix, llms.txt and the entity page, a G2 review drive and a measurement baseline. These cost days, not months.

### Conventions used on every page

- Every page opens with **"What this page is for"**, a **Last updated** date and a **Sources** line, and ends with **Related pages**.
- **TO CONFIRM with Quotr** = a fact the research could not settle; ask Quotr before using it.
- **UNVERIFIED** = reported (often by Perplexity) but not checked directly.
- **"Not re-checked"** = a third-party figure the fact-checks did not re-open; treat as "reported by X".
- **Recommendation / draft** = our suggestion, not a fact. Quotr must approve it.
- Test IDs (C1, V3, B4, P5…) refer to the September 2026 Perplexity prompts; T01–T53 are the monthly tracking set; R-01… are roadmap items; A1… C10 are 30-60-90 tasks.

## How to use it: AI tools

### Quick start (any tool)

1. Paste [[AI context pack]] at the start of the chat, or add it as the tool's standing instructions.
2. Attach the task-specific files from the table below.
3. Tell the tool: *"Use only facts marked High confidence in the fact sheet. Where a fact is missing or marked TO CONFIRM, write [TO CONFIRM] instead of guessing. Follow the style guide."*
4. Check every number in the output against [[Entity fact sheet]] before anything is published.

If a tool will not accept `.md` files, save a copy as `.txt`; the content is plain text. Upload and file-count limits change often, so check the tool's current limits. If you hit one, drop the long reference files first ([[Prompt library]], [[Buyer questions by trade]], [[Sources]], [[Website audit]]).

## How to keep it current: the monthly routine

Do this in the first week of each month. It takes about half a day, plus the prompt runs (3 hours for the Lite routine; use a tool for the Standard routine).

1. **Re-run the tracking set.** Follow [[Tracking set]] §3: the same 53 prompts, the same session rules and the same scoring. October 2026 is the first multi-engine month: run all six tools that month, then Claude and Copilot once a quarter.
2. **Update the baseline and dashboard.** Add the month's numbers to [[KPIs and dashboard]], re-score [[Presence scorecard]], and add a dated results section to [[AI visibility baseline]] (keep the September 2026 section unchanged as the "before" picture). Log any new outside page that names Quotr in [[Off-site presence]].
3. **Update the fact sheet.** When Quotr confirms a **TO CONFIRM** item or a fact changes (price, plans, customers, funding), change [[Entity fact sheet]] first. Then search the whole folder for the old value (for example "220+" or "Berkeley") and fix every page, including [[AI context pack]] and the TO CONFIRM list in [[QA log]].
4. **Check what was fixed on quotr.ai.** Tick off finished items in [[Optimize vs create]] (fact-fix sweep) and [[30-60-90 plan]], and note any page that went live.
5. **Log the changes.** Add a dated entry to [[Changelog]]: what changed, why, which files, and who checked it. Update "Last updated" on every page you touched.
6. **Check the links.** Ask a developer to run the link check described in [[QA log]], or ask an AI tool to list any relative links that point to missing files. The target is zero broken links.
7. **Every quarter,** also re-check the competitor profiles (prices, reviews, funding), the tool prices in [[AI visibility tools compared]], and the evidence in [[Signals that matter]]. AI search changes fast, so date every new study you add.
