## 2. Naming rules and what NOT to say

- First mention: **Quotr.ai**. Never "Quotr AI", "QUOTR", "Quoter" or "Quotr.io" (except "formerly Quotr.io").
- Write "the Quotr.ai Lite plan", not "Lite" alone: Kreo also sells plans called Lite and Plus.
- **Never** use the retired plans "Solo", "Team", "1 User Plan", "2–10 Users Plan", or "from $299.90". That old pricing is still on about 13 Quotr URLs and in llms.txt, and AI answers repeat it.
- Quotr.ai is **not** the Quotr Pro app (a different developer's App Store app, 37 ratings / 4.7). Never quote its ratings. Never link to namesakes (Quotr Pro, quotrhq.com, getquotr.com, quotr.software).
- Don't call Quotr a quote, quotation or invoicing app, or "a procurement program, not software".
- Don't write "PitchBook shows a $190K seed": that figure appears only in Perplexity answers.
- Don't claim reviews, awards, press coverage or association membership. G2 reportedly shows about 0 reviews (UNVERIFIED); no Capterra listing exists; no earned trade press was found (one paid EIN Presswire release, Feb 2025, launched the free FireTips LA fire-rebuild tool).
- Don't rank Quotr #1 in lists Quotr publishes. Don't write text addressed to AI or search engines.
- Competitor facts: PlanSwift and On-Screen Takeoff belong to **ConstructConnect** (not Trimble). STACK has about 1,400 reviews on Capterra and under 100 on G2.

1. **The only AI takeoff and estimating platform found that also sells factory-direct materials, delivered duty-paid.** Rivals' bundles are domestic retail (Handoff with Lowe's, Buildxact with dealers and Home Depot).
2. **Three ways to buy:** do it yourself (Software), done for you (Service), buy the materials (Procurement).
3. **Transparent, low entry price:** $79.90 per seat per month, with published per-sq-ft service prices.
4. An **AI Agent** for plain-English questions about the drawings (a supporting point: many rivals now claim AI chat).
5. **Founder story:** an architect and an engineer closing the design-to-cost gap.

## 7. Content rules (style guide essentials)

- Answer first (40–80 words), then question headings and self-contained sections.
- Put **"Quotr.ai" inside the fact sentence**: "Quotr.ai's Estimation Service charges $0.25 per sq ft for projects under 50,000 sq ft."
- Every number gets a source, a "Quotr data" label or a customer name, plus an "as of" date.
- Use tables for comparisons, named human authors, honest limits, and fair, dated competitor facts.
- Every new page must contain something no competitor or AI could write without Quotr's data, customers or people.
- Don't change the date without real changes. Put a year in a title only when the content was truly updated; never in URL slugs.
- Avoid hype, keyword stuffing, hidden text, instructions to AI and self-ranked "best of" lists.

## 9. GEO evidence in one paragraph

GEO is mostly good SEO plus other people talking about you. Google's May 2026 guide says no special files, markup or chunking are needed; publish "valuable, unique, non-commodity content". C-SEO Bench (NeurIPS 2025) found most GEO content tricks "largely ineffective". For SaaS, 82.3% of top-cited sources were third-party sites, mostly social, community and video (Aleyda Solis, Aug 2026). YouTube mentions are the strongest *correlate* of AI visibility (Ahrefs). Self-ranked lists carry risk (Lily Ray). llms.txt and AI-specific schema are weak signals.
