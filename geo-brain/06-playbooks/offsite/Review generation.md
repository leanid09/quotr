## 1. Why reviews matter for AI visibility

- **Review directories feed "alternatives" answers.** Capterra and G2 were the two most-cited sites in Quotr's 32 unbranded Perplexity test questions (9 answers each). For "Togal.AI alternatives" and "PlanSwift alternatives", Perplexity took its lead picks from G2 and Capterra alternatives pages (visibility tests §2; two runs in one session, not reproduced in the fact-check). A product with no reviews rarely appears on those pages.
- **List editors rank by review data.** ConstructConnect's August 2026 guide to AI takeoff tools built its comparison tables from dated G2 and Capterra ratings (offsite notes §1). A tool with zero reviews is "structurally excluded".
- **Trust prompts:** asked whether Quotr.ai is legit, Perplexity said it "did not find independent third-party reviews" (offsite notes §5), and in the "Is Quotr.ai legit? Quotr reviews" test it borrowed the unrelated Quotr Pro app's 37 ratings / 4.7 score instead (verification claim 30; reproduced in re-run B3). Real Quotr.ai reviews give AI something correct to quote.
- **One network now:** G2 agreed to acquire Capterra, Software Advice and GetApp from Gartner (announced January 2026; reportedly closed February 5, 2026), so one vendor setup and review programme may now feed all four directories (verification claim 37a). **Confirm the current onboarding path with G2.**
- **Keep it in proportion:**
  - G2's own survey (March 2026, 1,076 B2B software buyers) found 45% say a review-site citation is the most confidence-inspiring signal in an AI answer (50% of daily AI users). G2 is an interested party (`verification_geo_evidence.md` claim #20, H10).
  - A G2-commissioned Growth Memo study found user-generated content (Reddit, forums, YouTube) out-cites review sites at every buyer stage, and Aleyda Solis's August 2026 data put news/review sites at only 10.4% of top cited SaaS sources (`verification_geo_evidence.md` claim #18, H10).
  - So reviews are **essential hygiene and an enabler** (alternatives pages, list editors, trust answers), not the whole off-site strategy.

## 3. Set-up (week 1)

1. **Claim and rename the G2 profile.** Claim the existing quotr-io listing through G2's vendor portal (every company can claim a free profile; claims are reviewed by G2, reportedly within 1–3 business days). Ask G2 to change the product name to **Quotr.ai** and, if possible, the URL slug. Add: the canonical one-line description from the [fact sheet](../../00-quotr/entity-fact-sheet.md), current pricing (Lite $79.90 / Plus $299.90 per seat per month / Enterprise custom, 7-day trial), screenshots, the demo video, and a link to quotr.ai.
2. **Pick categories that feed the right pages:** takeoff, construction estimating, bid management and, if G2 has one that fits, construction procurement (**check which categories exist**). Category choice decides which "alternatives" and grid pages Quotr can appear on.
3. **Create the Capterra / GetApp / Software Advice listing** through the route G2 now offers for these former Gartner Digital Markets sites, which G2 now owns (**confirm the onboarding path with G2**), with the same description, categories and prices.
4. **Fix the other profiles:** claim F6S and replace the Revit copy; re-categorise Product Hunt from "Real estate" to construction/AI; add free listings on SourceForge and Slashdot (both appear in AI citation lists; see [../../03-market/citation-sources-map.md](../../03-market/citation-sources-map.md)).
5. **Get the official review links** from each platform's vendor portal. Use only these links.
6. **Name an owner** (one person) for the programme and the review replies.

## 4. Who to ask, and when

### 4a. Who

- **Every active customer user** who has used Quotr.ai for real work (estimators, owners, project managers). Named customers on the site: RL Electric, AlphaX, BiltWise Structures, Salisbury Moore; the full list is **TO CONFIRM with Quotr**.
- Quotr Service clients can review the software they used; a done-for-you service may not fit G2's software categories (**check with G2**).
- **Never:** employees, founders, investors (e.g., Llama Ventures), contractors paid by Quotr, family members, or anyone who has not used the product.

### 4b. When (ask everyone at the same milestones)

| Milestone | Why it works |
|---|---|
| 30–60 days after first paid use | Enough experience for a useful review |
| After the first bid or proposal sent from Quotr.ai | A concrete outcome to describe |
| After onboarding or training is completed (Plus/Enterprise) | Fresh, detailed experience |
| After a Quotr Service estimate is delivered and used | Real result to describe |
| At renewal | Long-term view |

**Important:** apply the milestone to **all** users who reach it. Do **not** filter by NPS score, satisfaction survey or "happy customers only". G2 prohibits collecting reviews "by any method that segments out potentially negative reviews or exclusively targets positive reviews" ([G2 Community Guidelines](https://legal.g2.com/community-guidelines), via WebSearch summary). That practice is called **review gating**, and it is banned.

### 4c. How many asks

- One request plus one reminder a week later. No more.
- A founder's personal note gets better responses than a mass email (common practice).

## 5. Scripts (edit the brackets)

**Email from a founder (primary):**

> Subject: A quick favour — your honest review of Quotr.ai
>
> Hi [First name],
>
> Thanks for using Quotr.ai on [project / for the last two months]. Other estimators and contractors decide which tools to trust by reading reviews from people like you.
>
> Would you write an honest review of Quotr.ai on G2? Good or bad, we want it all: it helps other contractors, and it tells us what to fix. It takes about 5–10 minutes:
> [official G2 review link]
>
> G2 checks that reviewers are real users, so it may ask you to sign in with LinkedIn or a work email.
> [Only if a G2-run incentive campaign is active: "G2 is offering reviewers a [$X gift card] for completing an approved review. It does not depend on what you write."]
>
> Thank you,
> [Hanyang Liu, CEO / Junzhe Shi, CTO], Quotr.ai

**In-app message (at the milestone):**

> You've sent your first proposal from Quotr.ai. Would you share an honest review on G2? It helps other contractors choose tools, and your feedback, positive or negative, helps us improve. [Write a review]

**Phone or meeting close (customer success):**

> "Would you be open to writing an honest review on G2 or Capterra about your experience? Whatever you think is fine. I'll send you the link."

**Reminder (one week later):**

> Hi [First name], just a gentle reminder in case the link got buried: [link]. If you'd rather not, no problem at all.

**Words to avoid:** "a 5-star review", "a positive review", "if you're happy", "say how much you love…", or any reward that depends on what the review says.

## 6. Rules the team must follow

### 6a. The law (United States)

The FTC's **Trade Regulation Rule on the Use of Consumer Reviews and Testimonials** took effect on **October 21, 2024** ([FTC Q&A](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/consumer-reviews-testimonials-rule-questions-answers)). It prohibits, among other things:
- **Fake reviews:** reviews by people who do not exist (including AI-generated ones), who did not use the product, or that misrepresent their experience; and buying or selling such reviews.
- **Sentiment-conditioned incentives:** giving anything of value in exchange for a review that expresses a particular sentiment (positive or negative).
- **Review suppression:** certain practices that stop or hide negative reviews.
- **Insider reviews:** certain reviews by company insiders without clear disclosure of the connection; officers and managers are restricted from writing them.
- Civil penalties can be large (the FTC cited $51,744 per violation in 2024).

The FTC's Endorsement Guides also say employees who promote their employer's products on social media must disclose the employment relationship ([FTC endorsement guides Q&A](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/ftcs-endorsement-guides-what-people-are-asking)).

### 6b. G2

(Via WebSearch summaries of G2's guidelines and help pages, 2026-09-25; re-read before launch.)
- **No gating or cherry-picking** (see 4b). No discouraging negative reviews. No contacting reviewers to change a published review.
- **Incentives are allowed only if they do not depend on sentiment**, are disclosed to G2, and are labelled "incentivized" on the review. G2's guidelines cap the value of any incentive at **$100**. Safest route: use **G2-run review campaigns** rather than sending your own rewards.
- **Reviewers are validated** (LinkedIn, business email, or personal email plus a screenshot of product use); low-quality or inauthentic reviews are rejected or removed.

### 6c. Capterra (and its sister sites)

(Via WebSearch summaries of Capterra's Community Guidelines, 2026-09-25; re-read before launch.)
- Incentives run through **Capterra's own programme** (gift cards for approved reviews, distributed by Capterra), using the incentive link from the vendor portal.
- Vendors must accept all verified reviews from an incentivised campaign that meet the guidelines.
- No incentives for employees, affiliates, competitors, government/public-sector employees, or people whose employers forbid gifts.
- Incentivised reviews are marked on the site.

### 6d. On quotr.ai

- Do **not** add review stars or ratings from G2/Capterra to quotr.ai's schema markup (Google: don't aggregate reviews from other websites; organizations can't mark up self-serving reviews) ([../schema-markup-kit.md](../schema-markup-kit.md) §4).
- If you quote a review on the site, check G2's or Capterra's terms for reuse first, and quote it exactly with the reviewer's name/role as shown.

## 7. Replying to reviews

- Reply to **every** review within 3 business days (recommended).
- **Positive:** thank them by name; mention one specific thing they said; no sales pitch.
- **Negative:** thank them; acknowledge the problem plainly; say what you are doing about it; offer a direct contact. Never argue, and never ask them to change the review.
- Feed recurring complaints to the product team; when fixed, you may reply again saying so (without asking for an edit).

**Reply template (negative):**
> Thank you, [Name], for the honest feedback. You're right that [issue] slowed you down on [project type]. We've [fixed / are working on] it: [one line]. If you'd like, reach me directly at [email] and I'll make sure it's sorted. — [Name, role], Quotr.ai
