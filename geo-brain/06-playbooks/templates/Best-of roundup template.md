# Template: "Best Of" Roundup (with Honest-Inclusion Rules)

**What this page is for:** A template and rulebook for category roundups such as "best AI takeoff software for residential subcontractors", including when Quotr should *not* publish one, how to include Quotr.ai honestly, and the documented risks of self-serving lists.

**Last updated:** 2026-09-25

**Sources:** [geo_content_playbook_b2b.md](<../../../research_notes/Quotr GEO AEO strategy audit/geo_content_playbook_b2b.md>) (§1, §5), [geo_ai_citation_signals_2026.md](<../../../research_notes/Quotr GEO AEO strategy audit/geo_ai_citation_signals_2026.md>) (§2), [verification_geo_evidence.md](<../../../research_notes/Quotr GEO AEO strategy audit/verification_geo_evidence.md>) (claims #2, #12, #19, #21; H3; X10, X17), [quotr_ai_visibility_tests.md](<../../../research_notes/Quotr GEO AEO strategy audit/quotr_ai_visibility_tests.md>) (§1, C1–C15), [competitor_geo_benchmark.md](<../../../research_notes/Quotr GEO AEO strategy audit/competitor_geo_benchmark.md>) (§1–3), [quotr_onsite_content_audit.md](<../../../research_notes/Quotr GEO AEO strategy audit/quotr_onsite_content_audit.md>) (§2, §4), [quotr_offsite_presence.md](<../../../research_notes/Quotr GEO AEO strategy audit/quotr_offsite_presence.md>) (§2).

## 1. Read this first: the evidence cuts both ways

**Why roundups get cited:**
- "Best X" listicles were **43.8%** of the page types ChatGPT cited in an Ahrefs study of 26,283 source URLs, and self-promotional lists that ranked the publisher #1 were still cited (`geo_ai_citation_signals_2026.md` §2).
- HubSpot's State of AEO 2026 (vendor-run, directional) put listicles among the three formats averaging above a 65% citation rate (`verification_geo_evidence.md` claim #12).
- "Best of" queries triggered a Google AI Overview **81.3%** of the time in Seer's 2026 data (`verification_geo_evidence.md` claim #21).

**Why self-serving roundups backfire:**
- In early 2026, several SaaS/B2B sites that ranked themselves first in "best of" lists lost **29–49%** of organic visibility within weeks (Lily Ray, Feb 3, 2026 post; Google has not confirmed a targeted update). Mass-produced "best X" lists and posts "lightly refreshed with '2026' in the title" were among the hardest hit (`verification_geo_evidence.md` claim #19).
- When a company's own self-promotional list was cited in AI Overviews, **that company was left out of the actual recommendation 69% of the time** (Lily Ray, June 17, 2026). Ranking yourself #1 can end up promoting your competitors.
- Lost Google visibility can carry into ChatGPT, Perplexity and others that lean on Google's index (`geo_content_playbook_b2b.md` §1).

**What happened to Quotr's roundups in the tests:**
- Quotr was named in **0 of 15** category prompts on Perplexity (and in 1 of 32 unbranded prompts overall, about 3%, while STACK, PlanSwift and Buildxact were named in 10 each), even though it has about 16 "best of" and buyer's-guide posts (visibility tests §1; onsite audit §2). Only Perplexity was tested.
- In one WebSearch check, Quotr's roundup ranked **4th of 9** for "best AI takeoff software for subcontractors 2026", yet Perplexity did not use it and recommended other tools (visibility tests C1; the ranking was not re-checked in the fact-check).
- The category is saturated: 15+ vendors publish dated "best AI takeoff" lists, most ranking themselves first (competitor benchmark §2, §5).

**Bottom line (from the verification file, X10):** a few honest, well-maintained pages are fine; **no library of self-ranked "best X" pages**; most effort goes into getting onto **third-party** lists ([../offsite/listicle-and-pr-outreach.md](../offsite/listicle-and-pr-outreach.md)).

## 2. Decision: should this roundup exist at all?

Answer these before writing. If you answer "no" to any of questions 1–4, do not publish a new roundup.

| # | Question | If "no" |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Is there a High-priority prompt in the [prompt library](../../04-prompt-library/prompt-library.md) with no good Quotr page? | Improve an existing page instead |
| 2 | Does Quotr already have a roundup on this topic? (It has ~16.) | Refresh or consolidate that one ([../page-refresh-checklist.md](../page-refresh-checklist.md)) |
| 3 | Can the page be genuinely fair, including tools that beat Quotr for some buyers? | Write a buyer's guide instead (criteria + checklist, no ranking) |
| 4 | Can we test or verify every tool on the list (vendor site, trial, published reviews)? | Narrow the list |
| 5 | Is this a segment where AI answers name no clear leader (e.g., residential + procurement, multifamily developers)? | If "yes", it is a stronger candidate (see [../../03-market/white-space.md](../../03-market/white-space.md)) |

**Better formats in many cases:**
- **Buyer's guide:** "How to choose AI takeoff software for residential subcontractors" with criteria, questions to ask vendors, a scoring sheet, and a neutral table. No rank.
- **Segment page:** "AI takeoff software for small residential subcontractors" (Ramp-style segment pages worked in a 2025 vendor case study: Ramp's AP visibility rose from 3.2% to 22.2% in one month after it published segment and comparison pages; labelled a vendor case study in `verification_geo_evidence.md` H14).

## 3. Honest-inclusion rules (non-negotiable)

1. **Disclose** at the top: "Quotr.ai publishes this list and Quotr.ai is one of the tools included."
2. **Publish the method:** criteria, how each tool was checked (trial, demo, vendor site, reviews), and the date.
3. **No numbered ranking with Quotr.ai at #1.** Use "best for [use case]" groupings. If a ranking is unavoidable, it must follow the published criteria, and Quotr.ai cannot win categories where it lacks evidence (e.g., "most reviewed").
4. **Include the tools buyers actually see in AI answers** for this category (for residential AI takeoff in Quotr's tests: Handoff, Buildxact, Togal.AI, STACK, Kreo, Bluebeam, PlanSwift). Explain any exclusion.
5. **Same fields for every tool,** including a "limitations" line for Quotr.ai.
6. **Every price, rating and review count is sourced and dated.** No unsourced negatives about competitors.
7. **No affiliate links or paid placements** unless disclosed next to the link.
8. **Year in the title only when every entry was re-checked that year.** Never bump the year without changes (`verification_geo_evidence.md` X17). No year in the slug.
9. **One list per topic.** No per-trade or per-city clones with the same content (scaled content abuse; `verification_geo_evidence.md` claim #2).
10. **Quarterly review** of every entry; update or remove stale entries.

## 5. Required sections, in order

| # | Section | What goes in it |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **H1** | "Best [category] for [segment]" (+ year only if fully re-checked) |
| 2 | **Byline, dates, disclosure** | Named author with relevant credential; disclosure line |
| 3 | **Quick answer** | 60–100 words: the best option per use case, with dated starting prices |
| 4 | **How we chose** | 5–8 criteria, how each tool was checked, date |
| 5 | **Summary table** | Tool / best for / starting price (dated) / free trial / key strength / key limitation / reviews (dated) |
| 6 | **One H2 per tool** | Same fields for all: what it is, best for, strengths, limitations, price, source links |
| 7 | **Buyer's checklist** | Questions to ask any vendor (accuracy on your plans, scanned PDFs, exports, integrations, data security, trial) |
| 8 | **How to choose** | "If you… → consider…" decision table |
| 9 | **FAQ** | 4–8 questions ([faq-block.md](faq-block.md)) |
| 10 | **Sources and method** | Links, dates, correction contact |

## 6. Filled-in example outline: "AI takeoff software for residential subcontractors"

**H1:** AI Takeoff Software for Residential Subcontractors: 7 Options Compared

**Disclosure:** Quotr.ai publishes this list and Quotr.ai is one of the tools included. We checked each vendor's website and pricing page on [date]; review counts are from G2 or Capterra on that date.

**How we chose (draft criteria):** works on residential PDF plan sets; AI counting and measuring; published price; free trial; estimating after takeoff; review evidence; support for small teams.

**Summary table (draft; re-check every cell):**

| Tool | Best for | Starting price (as of [date]) | Free trial | Reviews (as of [date]) | Key limitation |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Handoff | Residential remodelers and builders who want CRM, scheduling and invoicing too | Flex $119/month billed annually (handoff.ai/pricing) | 7 days | G2 ~115 (search summary) | [sourced] |
| Buildxact | Small-to-mid residential builders; digital takeoff with live dealer pricing | [check buildxact.com] | [check] | Capterra 4.6 from 150+ (Buildxact newsroom) | [sourced] |
| Togal.AI | AI-first takeoff, multi-trade subs | Growth $299/user/month billed yearly | No self-serve trial button | G2 4.8 from 60 (July 2026) | [sourced] |
| STACK | Cloud takeoff and estimating; has a free version | From $249/user/month | Free version | Capterra ~1,400; G2 <100 | [sourced] |
| Kreo | Lowest entry price for AI-assisted 2D takeoff | Lite $35/user/month billed annually | 7 days | G2 4.5 from 32 | [sourced] |
| Bluebeam Revu | PDF-centred teams doing manual takeoff | Tiers from ~$260/user/year (re-check) | [check] | G2 ~461 | [sourced] |
| Quotr.ai | Takeoff → estimate → bid in one tool, with optional estimating service and factory-direct materials | Lite $79.90/seat/month | 7 days | No verified third-party reviews yet | Small review base; integrations TO CONFIRM |

**H2 for Quotr.ai (same format as the others):**
- *What it is:* "Quotr.ai is AI construction takeoff, estimating and bid software, with a done-for-you estimating service and factory-direct material procurement."
- *Best for:* residential subs and builders who want the takeoff to flow into an estimate, a bid comparison and a proposal, and who may want to buy finish materials factory-direct.
- *Strengths:* AI Agent for questions about the drawings; estimating with your own or Quotr's cost database; supplier quote comparison; Estimation Service at $0.25/$0.10 per sq ft.
- *Limitations:* few third-party reviews so far; integrations and supported file types beyond PDF/image **TO CONFIRM**.
- *Price:* Lite $79.90, Plus $299.90 per seat per month; Enterprise custom; 7-day free trial (as of September 2026).

## 7. What to do with Quotr's existing roundups

Quotr has about 16 best-of and buyer's-guide posts (e.g., best-ai-construction-estimating-software-2026, best-ai-bid-software-for-construction, best-electrical-estimating-software-2026, best-drywall-estimating-software-in-2026, best-concrete-estimating-software-2026, best-plumbing-estimating-software-2026, best-flooring-estimating-software-in-2026, best-glazing-estimating-software-2026, hvac-estimating-software-2026-buyers-guide; onsite audit §2).

1. **Fix facts first:** several carry the retired "Solo $299.90 / Team $499.90" pricing (verification, gaps filled #2).
2. **Apply the honest-inclusion rules:** remove self-ranking at #1; add disclosure, method and limitations.
3. **Consolidate overlaps** (e.g., best-ai-bid-software-for-construction and ai-bidding-software-construction; the two buyer's guides) where they target the same prompt.
4. **Do not add new trade roundups** until the existing ones pass the rubric in [../page-refresh-checklist.md](../page-refresh-checklist.md).
