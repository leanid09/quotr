# Template: Alternatives Page ("[Competitor] Alternatives")

**What this page is for:** A reusable template for "[Competitor] alternatives" pages that help buyers leaving a named tool, list real options fairly (including Quotr.ai where it fits), and avoid the self-promotional patterns that search engines and AI answers now discount.

**Last updated:** 2026-09-25

**Sources:** <../../../research_notes/Quotr GEO AEO strategy audit/quotr_ai_visibility_tests.md> (§2, V1–V10), <../../../research_notes/Quotr GEO AEO strategy audit/competitor_geo_benchmark.md> (§1–4), <../../../research_notes/Quotr GEO AEO strategy audit/quotr_offsite_presence.md> (§2), <../../../research_notes/Quotr GEO AEO strategy audit/geo_content_playbook_b2b.md> (§1), <../../../research_notes/Quotr GEO AEO strategy audit/quotr_onsite_content_audit.md> (§2, §4), <../../../research_notes/Quotr GEO AEO strategy audit/verification_geo_evidence.md> (claims #2, #19; X10), <../../../research_notes/Quotr GEO AEO strategy audit/verification_quotr_and_competitors.md> (claims 26–27, 32; gaps filled #5); competitor profiles in [../../03-market/competitors/](../../03-market/competitors/).

## 1. Purpose and what the evidence says

- **Purpose:** answer "What can I use instead of [Tool]?" for buyers who already know a competitor. These buyers are close to a decision.
- **What AI engines cite for these prompts** (Quotr's September 2026 tests, Perplexity):
  - **Review-directory "alternatives" pages** lead: G2 and Capterra alternatives pages supplied the lead picks for "Togal.AI alternatives" and "PlanSwift alternatives" (visibility tests V1, V3). G2 now owns Capterra, GetApp and Software Advice (verification claim 37a), so review volume there matters a lot.
  - **Vendor-written alternatives posts** fill the rest (costlogic, oneestimate, easytakeoffs, projul, struvia, Quotr's own).
- **What happens to Quotr's own alternatives posts today:**
  - They rank. Quotr's posts took results 5–7 for "Togal.AI alternatives 2026" in WebSearch (offsite notes §2).
  - Perplexity **cited** Quotr's PlanSwift post for facts about PlanSwift but **did not recommend Quotr** (V3, both runs). In "Togal.AI alternatives", Quotr appeared near the bottom (about 15th of 17 brands, then 8th of 9) (V1).
  - Two near-duplicate Togal-alternatives posts are both cited (competitor benchmark §4).
- **The risks:**
  - "Scaled competitor 'alternatives' pages" were among the patterns hit in early 2026 (29–49% visibility drops at some SaaS/B2B sites; Lily Ray's observations; Google has not confirmed an update) (`verification_geo_evidence.md` claim #19).
  - The category is saturated: 15+ vendors publish "best X / X alternatives 2026" posts, many ranking themselves first (competitor benchmark §2).
- **Conclusion for Quotr:** keep a **small number** of alternatives pages for competitors that matter; make each one genuinely useful and fair; consolidate duplicates; spend more effort getting onto G2/Capterra alternatives pages (see [../offsite/review-generation.md](../offsite/review-generation.md)).

## 3. Honest-inclusion rules (apply to every alternatives page)

1. **Disclose** at the top: "Quotr.ai publishes this page and is one of the options listed."
2. **Organise by use case, not by rank.** Use "Best for residential remodelers", "Best for commercial precon teams", "Lowest entry price", not "#1, #2, #3".
3. **Include the tools buyers actually consider.** Use the brands AI engines and directories already name for this competitor (see the example below). Leaving out obvious options makes the page look like an ad.
4. **Quotr.ai gets the same treatment as everyone else:** same fields, same "limitations" row, same sourcing.
5. **Say why people leave the competitor, with sources** (G2/Capterra review themes, a pricing change on the vendor's own site). No unsourced knocks.
6. **Every price, rating and review count is dated** ("as of September 2026") and linked.
7. **One page per competitor.** No per-trade or per-city variants unless each has genuinely different content (Google: pages built for every query variation can be scaled content abuse; `verification_geo_evidence.md` claim #2).
8. **Re-check quarterly** (prices and reviews change fast).

## 4. Required sections, in order

| # | Section | What goes in it |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **H1** | "[Competitor] Alternatives: [N] Options by Use Case" (add the year only when every fact was re-checked that year; no year in the slug) |
| 2 | **Byline, dates, disclosure** | Named author; Published / Last updated; disclosure line |
| 3 | **Quick answer** | 60–90 words: the 3–4 strongest alternatives by use case, including Quotr.ai only where it fits, with one dated price each |
| 4 | **Why people look for alternatives to [Competitor]** | 3–5 sourced reasons (price, platform, missing features, review themes) |
| 5 | **How we chose** | Criteria (e.g., AI takeoff, price transparency, free trial, residential fit, estimating, reviews) and date checked |
| 6 | **Comparison table** | Tool / best for / starting price (dated) / free trial / AI takeoff / estimating / reviews (dated) / main limitation |
| 7 | **One H2 per alternative** | "[Tool]: best for [use case]" → what it is (1 sentence), strengths, limitations, price (dated, linked), who should pick it |
| 8 | **Where Quotr.ai fits** | Same format as the others, including its limitations (e.g., few third-party reviews yet) |
| 9 | **How to choose** | Decision table: "If you… → consider…" |
| 10 | **Switching checklist** | What to export from the old tool, how to trial the new one |
| 11 | **FAQ** | 4–8 questions (see [faq-block.md](faq-block.md)) |
| 12 | **Sources** | Every link, with the date checked |
| 13 | **About Quotr.ai** | Approved boilerplate |

## 6. Optional: an honest "Quotr.ai alternatives" page (prompt D-023)

Buyers will ask "Quotr alternatives". Today no page answers it, so AI engines fill the gap from whatever they find. A fair page written by Quotr can shape that answer, **but only if it is genuinely fair**:
- Name the tools a Quotr buyer would really consider (Togal.AI, STACK, Handoff, Buildxact, Kreo, Beam AI), with who each suits better than Quotr.
- State plainly when Quotr.ai is not the right fit (e.g., if you need CRM and scheduling, or a large review base).
- Treat this as a later-stage idea; do it after the fact fixes and the main comparison pages. Decide with Quotr.
