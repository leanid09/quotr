**What this page is for:** A template for customer case studies that lead with measured, dated results, so buyers, list editors and AI engines have verifiable proof about Quotr.ai instead of vendor claims.

## 1. Why numbers-first case studies matter for Quotr

- **Quotr's proof is thin today.** Four case studies exist (RL Electric, AlphaX, BiltWise Structures, Salisbury Moore). The RL Electric page's "measurable outcomes" are qualitative ("AI-assisted", "Reduced", "Dozens"); the one number ("20 hours … 1–2 hours") appears only in a homepage testimonial. Testimonials use first names only, and one "customer perspective" is from the seed investor (onsite audit §3–4, §6).
- **AI engines already discount Quotr's claims.** On "Is Quotr.ai legit? reviews", Perplexity said it "did not find independent third-party reviews" and treated Quotr's claims as "vendor assertions" (offsite notes §5).
- **Construction buyers want accuracy evidence.** In the Dodge/CMiC survey, data accuracy (57%) was the top AI concern, and only 19% of contractors had adapted workflows (`geo_content_playbook_b2b.md` §6).
- **Specific numbers and dates are what get quoted:** DATE and NUMBER entities best predicted ChatGPT citations (Growth Memo, June 2026; not re-checked).
- **Competitors publish customer numbers:** e.g., Togal cites NC Painting raising bids to about 60 a month, a "215% increase" (competitor benchmark §2).
- **A case study also feeds off-site work:** list editors, journalists and review-site profiles all ask for customer proof ([../offsite/listicle-and-pr-outreach.md](../offsite/listicle-and-pr-outreach.md)).

## 3. Rules for case-study numbers

1. **Measure before and after the same way** (same task, same kind of project). State how it was measured (timesheets, software logs, invoices).
2. **Give the period** ("March–August 2026") and the sample ("12 bids").
3. **Use the customer's full name and title** in quotes, with written permission. First names only look unverifiable.
4. **Label who is speaking.** An investor, partner or employee is not a customer. The homepage's "Customer perspective: Kyle, Llama Ventures" must be relabelled or replaced (Llama Ventures is Quotr's seed investor).
5. **No invented or "typical" results.** The FTC's rule on consumer reviews and testimonials (in force since October 21, 2024) bans testimonials that misrepresent the giver's experience and requires clear disclosure of material connections for insider testimonials ([FTC Q&A](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/consumer-reviews-testimonials-rule-questions-answers)). If the customer received a discount or other benefit for participating, disclose it.
6. **Check the maths.** "20 hours to 1–2 hours" is a 90–95% cut, not "up to 80%".
7. **Show what did not change** or what the customer still does by hand. It makes the rest credible.

## 4. Required sections, in order

| # | Section | What goes in it |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **H1** | "[Customer] cut [metric] from [A] to [B] with Quotr.ai" (numbers in the title) |
| 2 | **Results box** | 3–4 numbers, each with unit, period and how it was measured |
| 3 | **Customer snapshot table** | Company, trade, region, team size, project types, Quotr products used (Software / Service / Procurement, plan), customer since |
| 4 | **The problem** | Baseline numbers: hours per takeoff, bids per month, win rate, material cost; what was at stake |
| 5 | **Why they chose Quotr.ai** | What else they considered (named tools, fairly) and why |
| 6 | **How they use Quotr.ai** | Workflow, 2–4 screenshots (with permission), the AI Agent or features used |
| 7 | **Results in detail** | Before/after table; method; period |
| 8 | **What didn't change / limits** | Honest notes |
| 9 | **Customer quotes** | Full name, title, company; 1–3 quotes that include a number |
| 10 | **Video** | Embedded customer video with a text summary or transcript |
| 11 | **About [Customer]** | 2 sentences + link |
| 12 | **Related** | Trade page, pricing, similar case studies |

**Structure all case studies the same way** so the /case-studies/ hub can show a comparable results table across customers.
