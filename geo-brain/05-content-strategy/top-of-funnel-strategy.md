# Top-of-Funnel Strategy: Keeping and Growing Reach When AI Answers Take the Clicks

**What this page is for:** How Quotr.ai keeps and grows its top-of-funnel reach (people at the early research stage who are not yet shopping for a specific product) now that AI answers in Google, ChatGPT and other engines answer many questions without a click. It covers what is happening to clicks, how to redefine "reach", seven moves to grow it, which assets still earn clicks and email sign-ups, and how to measure it. It answers the meeting question **"How do we maintain and expand top-of-funnel reach?"**

**Last updated:** 2026-09-25

**Sources:** the report [audit report](<../../reports/Quotr GEO AEO strategy audit.md>) (section "Top-of-funnel reach now means being named, not just clicked"); fact-checks [verification_geo_evidence.md](<../../research_notes/Quotr GEO AEO strategy audit/verification_geo_evidence.md>) (claims 6–8, 21–24; M1, M2, M4, M5, M9; hype flags H1, H8) and [verification_quotr_and_competitors.md](<../../research_notes/Quotr GEO AEO strategy audit/verification_quotr_and_competitors.md>) (AI prompt re-runs); research notes [geo_content_playbook_b2b.md](<../../research_notes/Quotr GEO AEO strategy audit/geo_content_playbook_b2b.md>) (§2, §4, §7), [geo_ai_citation_signals_2026.md](<../../research_notes/Quotr GEO AEO strategy audit/geo_ai_citation_signals_2026.md>) (§1: which search index each engine uses), [quotr_ai_visibility_tests.md](<../../research_notes/Quotr GEO AEO strategy audit/quotr_ai_visibility_tests.md>) (§3 how-to prompts), [competitor_geo_benchmark.md](<../../research_notes/Quotr GEO AEO strategy audit/competitor_geo_benchmark.md>) (§3, §5); brain pages [../03-market/white-space.md](../03-market/white-space.md), [../04-prompt-library/tracking-set.md](../04-prompt-library/tracking-set.md), [content-priorities.md](content-priorities.md), [content-roadmap.md](content-roadmap.md). ## The short answer

AI answers will keep taking clicks away from educational content. That is now shown by a randomized experiment, not just by correlation. So Quotr should measure top-of-funnel reach as **presence in AI answers** (being *named*, not just cited), plus visits to the assets people still click through to, plus growth in brand demand, and not only as blog sessions.

Quotr can grow reach in three ways (report):
1. **Show up in answers on the unclaimed topics:** factory-direct buying, tariffs and landed costs, multifamily costs and rebuild costs. Almost no software company is cited there today, and Quotr has the data.
2. **Build assets people still click through to:** original data indexes, free calculators, downloadable templates and videos, with optional email capture.
3. **Be present where AI reads beyond quotr.ai:** reviews, independent lists, YouTube, Reddit and estimator communities, LinkedIn and trade press.

Underneath all three: classic SEO in Google **and Bing** (plus Brave), consistent facts, and key sentences written so the name "Quotr.ai" travels with the fact.

## Words used on this page

- **Top of funnel (TOFU):** early-stage research ("how to estimate drywall for a house", "how are tariffs affecting material costs"). **MOFU:** comparing options. **BOFU:** checking Quotr itself.
- **Zero-click search:** a search that ends without a click to any website.
- **AI Overviews (AIO):** AI summaries at the top of Google results. **AI Mode:** Google's chat-style search.
- **Named vs cited:** named = the AI says "Quotr" in the answer; cited = a quotr.ai page appears as a source link.
- **Click-resilient content:** content people still need to visit after reading the AI answer, for example to use a tool, download a file or check live numbers (Aleyda Solis's framework, [content prioritization in an AI search era](https://www.aleydasolis.com/en/ai-search/content-prioritization-ai-search/)).
- **Branded search:** searches that include the brand name ("quotr.ai pricing").
- **GSC:** Google Search Console. **GA4:** Google Analytics 4.

## What is happening to top-of-funnel clicks (the evidence)

| Evidence | What it says | Source and caveat |
|---|---|---|
| **First randomized experiment** (preprint, Aug 18, 2026; about 1,100 Google users) | Moving people to Google AI Mode cut clicks to outside websites by **18.8 percentage points**. Removing AI Overviews **raised** clicks by **8.8 points**. Neither improved perceived usefulness or trust | [arXiv 2608.18352](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.18352v1); not yet peer-reviewed |
| **Zero-click searches** | **68%** of US Google searches ended without a click in early 2026 (Jan–Apr, Similarweb panel) | [SparkToro](https://sparktoro.com/blog/in-2026-less-than-one-third-of-google-searches-still-send-a-click/) |
| **Scale of Google's AI features** | AI Mode passed **1 billion** monthly users and AI Overviews **2.5 billion** (May 2026). Follow-up questions from an AI Overview now continue in AI Mode | [Google I/O 2026](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/products/search/search-io-2026/) |
| **Being cited helps, but does not undo the loss** | On AI Overview queries, cited pages got **120% more clicks per view** than uncited pages, but still **38% fewer** than pages on searches with no AI Overview | [Seer Interactive](https://www.seerinteractive.com/insights/aio-impact-on-google-ctr-2026-update) (Apr 2026; 53 brands); correlation, not cause |
| **Which queries get AI Overviews** | About 36% of informational queries, 8% of commercial and 5% of transactional. But "X vs Y" queries **95.4%**, "best of" 81.3%, price and cost 83.4%. On informational queries with **no** AI Overview, organic click-through actually **rose** during 2025 (2.93% → 3.97%) | Seer, same study |
| **ChatGPT now links brand names** | Since May 7, 2026, ChatGPT links brand names straight to their homepages. Profound (a vendor) says B2B software gained most: daily ChatGPT referrals up **more than 200%**. OpenAI can change this at any time | [Profound](https://www.tryprofound.com/blog/chatgpt-referrals-branded-links) |
| **ChatGPT often answers from memory** | ChatGPT reportedly searches the web on only about a third of prompts; the rest come from what the model already knows (so brand mentions across the web matter, not just pages) | Signals notes §1 (Nectiv and OtterlyAI figures; not re-checked) |
| **Do AI visitors buy?** | The only peer-reviewed study (973 online stores) found ChatGPT visitors converted below every traditional channel except paid social, but did better for **complex products** and were improving over time | [Marketing Science, Apr 2026](https://pubsonline.informs.org/doi/10.1287/mksc.2025.0489); e-commerce, not B2B. Quotr should measure its own |
| **Buyers do start in AI** | G2's own survey: 51% of B2B software buyers start research in an AI chatbot; one-third bought from a vendor they had never heard of before | [G2](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/new-g2-research-half-of-b2b-software-buyers-now-start-their-research-with-ai-chatbots-302742807.html); G2 sells reviews, so directional |
| **Placements decay** | Only about a third of AI-cited pages were still cited 28 days later | [Digital Authority Partners](https://www.digitalauthority.me/resources/ai-visibility-study/) |

**Construction-specific data is missing:** no study measures AI Overview prevalence or click loss for estimating and takeoff queries (playbook notes §7 gaps). Quotr's own Search Console and GA4 data will be the best evidence within one or two quarters.

## Redefine "reach": three layers

| Layer | What it is | Why it matters now | Main measures |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Presence in AI answers** | Quotr is named (and ideally linked) in AI answers to early-stage questions | Many people read the answer and never click; being named is the new impression. ChatGPT now turns brand names into homepage links | Share of tracked prompts naming Quotr, per engine; GSC AI Overview/AI Mode impressions; Bing AI Performance citations |
| **2. Visits to click-resilient assets** | Tools, datasets, templates, videos | People still click to use, download or verify | Tool uses, downloads, dataset page views, AI-referred sessions (GA4) |
| **3. Brand demand** | People search for Quotr, visit directly, book demos | The downstream result of layers 1 and 2 plus off-site presence | Branded search volume, direct visits, demos with "found you via ChatGPT/Google AI" |

Keep blog sessions as a secondary number, not the headline top-of-funnel KPI.

## Seven moves to keep and grow reach

### Move 1. Be named, not just cited

**Problem:** AI already uses Quotr's pages as sources and drops the name. For outsourced-estimating prices (C12), Perplexity used Quotr's blog as its first source on both runs and credited the rates to "one outsourced estimating service" and "some firms". The same happened for AI takeoff accuracy (P5) and plumbing estimating (N2) (report; tracking set T12, T30, T49).

**What to do:**
- **Write the brand into the fact.** Examples:

| Instead of | Write |
|---|---|
| "Outsourced estimating typically costs $0.25 per sq ft for smaller projects." | "Quotr.ai's Estimation Service charges $0.25 per sq ft for projects under 50,000 sq ft and $0.10 per sq ft above that." |
| "AI takeoff reaches 94–99% on clean vector sets and drops into the 80s on scans." (how Perplexity summarized Quotr's accuracy post, without the name) | "In Quotr.ai's internal testing, AI takeoff counts reached [approved figure, e.g. 95–99%] accuracy on clean vector PDFs and dropped to roughly 80–88% on low-resolution scans." (Perplexity's summary said 94–99%, while other Quotr pages say 95–99%: use one approved figure, **TO CONFIRM with Quotr**; method page to follow) |
| "Our index found factory-direct tile was X% cheaper." | "Quotr.ai's Q4 2026 Factory-Direct vs US Dealer Price Index found …" (numbers **TO CONFIRM** when the index is built) |

- **Start with the pages AI already fetches** (list in [optimize-vs-create.md](optimize-vs-create.md), "Pages to refresh first").
- **Make the facts consistent everywhere** so engines stop hedging ("50+ to 220+ factories, depending on the page") and stop quoting the old $299.90 price. See the fact-fix sweep.
- **Get the same facts stated by others** (reviews, lists, press): AI accepts a recommendation more readily when third parties agree ([offsite-earned-media-plan.md](offsite-earned-media-plan.md)).
- **Name the thing, too:** give data products a stable, brandable name ("Quotr.ai Factory-Direct Price Index") so journalists and AI answers repeat it.

**Measure:** tracking prompts T12, T30, T49 move from "cited, not named" to "named".

### Move 2. Own the unclaimed topics with Quotr's own data

**Why:** the biggest top-of-funnel opportunity is where no software vendor is cited yet and Quotr holds data no rival has (report conclusion; [../03-market/white-space.md](../03-market/white-space.md)):

| Topic | Evidence it is open | Roadmap pieces |
|---|---|---|
| **Tariffs and landed cost** | Tariff answers cited only the Joint Economic Committee, Brookings, NAHB, Construction Dive, Skanska, Cushman & Wakefield, HousingWire | R-10, R-12, R-19, R-20, R-29 |
| **Factory-direct buying and importing** | P8 cited only sourcing agents and logistics firms; P4 never mentioned factory-direct as a lever; C10 and C14 named suppliers and marketplaces | R-07, R-08, R-09, R-11, R-30, R-31 |
| **Multifamily costs** | P3 named no software; small vendors Meltplan and Exayard get cited next to RSMeans for cost pages | R-40, R-53 |
| **Residential rebuild and California costs** | "Cost to rebuild after the LA fires" cited Bloomberg and local GCs; Quotr sells an LA rebuild estimate sample | R-21, R-55 |

This is also where Quotr can be the **named** source, because the facts are Quotr's own (a price index, a line-item house example), not generic knowledge. Full plan: [content-roadmap.md](content-roadmap.md), clusters 1–3.

**Guardrail:** the "best AI takeoff software" lists are crowded by 15-plus vendors. Top-of-funnel growth depends less on chasing them and more on becoming the named source for real construction cost numbers (report conclusion).

### Move 3. Build assets people still click through to (and capture email)

**Why:** tools and data are the most click-resilient formats. For "how to estimate drywall for a house" (P2), most sources were calculators, including free ones from three software vendors (Easy Takeoffs, Procore, BuildVision). A primary-research page earned about 3.3× more citations per page in Growth Memo's study.

**Assets (from the roadmap):**
- **Data:** Factory-Direct vs US Dealer Price Index (quarterly), tariff-per-house example, cost per sq ft by trade, AI takeoff accuracy benchmark, monthly lead-time tracker.
- **Calculators:** landed cost (DDP), drywall, flooring waste factor, cabinet run; later tile and concrete.
- **Templates:** bid-leveling spreadsheet, development pro forma material budget.
- **Video:** the "AI takeoff on real residential plans" series.

**Email capture from tools (design rules):**
1. **Never gate the answer.** Show calculator results and the headline data on the page, in plain HTML text, so people and AI can use them. Gating the answer removes the reason to cite the page.
2. **Offer an optional next step:** "Email me this estimate as a PDF", "Send me the full dataset (CSV)", "Get the quarterly price index and monthly lead-time note".
3. **One simple newsletter:** for example a monthly "Quotr.ai Cost Data" note (lead times, tariff changes, index updates). Short, dated, useful; also posted on LinkedIn.
4. **Tag the source** of every sign-up (which tool or dataset) in the CRM, so top-of-funnel assets can be tied to demos later.
5. **Ask "How did you hear about us?"** on demo and trial forms, with options such as ChatGPT, Google AI, Perplexity, YouTube, Reddit, LinkedIn, a review site, a colleague (report; playbook notes §4).
6. **Consent and privacy:** clear opt-in; follow the privacy policy on [/privacy/](https://quotr.ai/privacy/).

### Move 4. Keep classic SEO strong, in Google and in Bing (and Brave)

**Why:** GEO is mostly good SEO plus getting other people to talk about you (report). Google's May 2026 guide says a page only needs to be indexed and eligible to show in Search, and that "valuable, unique, non-commodity content" matters most ([Google](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/ai-optimization-guide)). The peer-reviewed C-SEO Bench found classic SEO "significantly more effective" than GEO content tricks ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.11097)). About 38% of pages cited in Google AI Overviews rank in the top 10 for the same search (Ahrefs, 2026). Each AI engine grounds its answers in a search index: Google's own; **Bing** for Copilot; for ChatGPT, unnamed "third-party search providers" plus its own crawl (OpenAI has not confirmed which; Bing is often assumed); **Brave** (the only search provider named in Anthropic's subprocessor list for Claude's web search, though Anthropic has not confirmed it is the only index); and Perplexity's own index (signals notes §1; GEO-evidence fact-check, claim 29).

**What to do:**
- **Fix the technical basics** (from the fact-fix sweep): add the blog sitemap to robots.txt, real lastmod dates, server-rendered blog index, remove the staging site from search, 301 old quotr.io pages ([optimize-vs-create.md](optimize-vs-create.md), section E).
- **Verify Quotr in Bing Webmaster Tools** and use IndexNow or URL submission after every important change; watch the AI Performance report.
- **Check Brave** occasionally (search the key pages there), since Claude's web search appears to use it.
- **Descriptive titles and URLs** that match the narrower questions people ask; Ahrefs found descriptive URLs were cited 89.78% of the times they appeared in ChatGPT results vs 81.11% for less descriptive ones ([Ahrefs](https://ahrefs.com/blog/why-chatgpt-cites-pages/); vendor study, not re-checked by the fact-check). Year-free URLs for recurring pages.
- **Hubs and internal links:** each cluster pillar links to its pieces and back ([content-roadmap.md](content-roadmap.md)).
- **Do not opt out** of Google's generative AI features. Google added a site-level opt-out in 2026; opted-out sites get no traffic or impressions from those surfaces (GEO-evidence fact-check, M1). Quotr needs the visibility.
- **Keep the answer-first format** Quotr already uses; it helps readers and AI alike.

### Move 5. Invest in YouTube

**Why:** YouTube was the largest single source site for SaaS brands in AI answers in Aleyda Solis's August 2026 study, and YouTube mentions are the strongest correlate of AI visibility in Ahrefs' 75,000-brand study (a correlation, not proof of cause). Perplexity never cited YouTube in our tests, so expect the effect mainly in Google's AI features and ChatGPT. Video is also click-resilient for "show me how" questions.

**What to do:** the "AI takeoff on real residential plans" series (V-01 to V-06), data explainers, customer jobs; "Quotr.ai" in titles and said aloud; every video embedded on its matching page with a text summary. Details: [offsite-earned-media-plan.md](offsite-earned-media-plan.md), section 6.

### Move 6. Be present in communities and on third-party sites

**Why:** for SaaS, 82.3% of top cited sources in AI Mode, Gemini and ChatGPT were other people's sites, most of them social, community and video (45.7%) (Aleyda Solis, as corrected in the GEO-evidence fact-check). Growth Memo reports that user-generated content absorbs roughly a third of the traffic AI Overviews leave behind (playbook notes §7; in a study commissioned by G2; not re-checked by the fact-check).

**What to do:** disclosed founder answers in r/estimators and estimator groups; reviews on G2 (feeding Capterra, GetApp and Software Advice); outreach to the lists AI cites; LinkedIn founder articles; trade-press data stories. Plan: [offsite-earned-media-plan.md](offsite-earned-media-plan.md).

### Move 7. Grow brand demand and make the first visit count

**Why:** as AI answers name brands and ChatGPT links brand names to homepages, more first-time visitors arrive on the **homepage** already knowing only the name. The homepage must explain Quotr.ai in one sentence and route people fast (report).

**What to do:**
- **Homepage:** a plain first line such as the draft boilerplate "Quotr.ai is AI construction takeoff, estimating and bid software, plus estimating services and factory-direct materials" (entity fact sheet §5, **draft for Quotr approval**); fix "A procurement program, not software…" wording, which led Perplexity to describe all of Quotr as a procurement program; links to the data hub and free tools in the navigation.
- **Brand-building events:** each data launch, press story, podcast and LinkedIn article is a reason for people to search "Quotr.ai".
- **Branded-search hygiene:** make sure searches for "Quotr.ai reviews" and "Quotr.ai pricing" land on correct pages (today the top web result for "Quotr.ai reviews" is the unrelated Quotr Pro app). Real reviews and a reviews hub fix this over time.
- **Name clarity:** always pair "Quotr.ai" with "construction takeoff and estimating" in titles and first sentences, to separate it from the eight-plus other "Quotr" products.

## Which top-of-funnel content to grow, keep lean or stop

Scored with Aleyda Solis's three questions: does the reader still need to click (click resilience), is the information unique and checkable (citation potential), and is there a natural reason to name Quotr (brand-mention potential)? Scores are our judgment.

| Content type | Risk of being fully summarized by AI | Click resilience | Brand-mention potential | What Quotr should do |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Original data indexes and benchmarks | Low (people check the table and method) | High | High (it is Quotr's data) | **Grow** (roadmap cluster 2) |
| Calculators and templates | Low | High | Medium–High ("Quotr.ai's free … calculator") | **Grow** |
| Line-item cost guides (LA rebuild, cabinets per linear foot, ADU) | Medium | Medium–High | High (Quotr's numbers) | **Grow** |
| Neutral procurement and tariff guides | Medium | Medium | Medium–High (Quotr is the practitioner) | **Grow** |
| Video walkthroughs on real plans | Low | High | High (name said aloud) | **Grow** |
| Trade how-tos with worked Quotr examples | Medium–High | Medium | Medium (if the brand is attached) | **Keep and refresh** |
| Generic "what is X" definitions | Very high | Low | Low | **Keep lean:** short, answer-first, linking to tools and data; do not add many more |
| Generic "what is AI takeoff" explainers | Very high (Bluebeam, Autodesk, Procore win) | Low | Low | **Keep lean** |
| Self-ranked "best X" listicles | High | Low | Low (the author is left out 69% of the time when cited in AI Overviews, per Lily Ray) | **Stop adding** |
| Event recaps | High | Low | Low | **Keep short** |

## Risks and caveats

- **One engine tested.** All Quotr baselines come from Perplexity's Sonar model. ChatGPT and Google AI Mode may behave differently; October's multi-engine baseline comes first (report caveat).
- **Volatility.** Citations churn (about a third survive 28 days) and platforms swing (Reddit's ChatGPT citation share fell 3.83% → 0.52% in weeks). Spread presence across channels and re-test monthly.
- **Correlation vs cause.** YouTube and brand-mention findings are correlations; the click-loss experiment is causal but a preprint; Seer's "cited pages get more clicks" is a correlation.
- **Conversion is unproven for B2B.** Measure Quotr's own AI-referred demos rather than assume AI traffic converts better (GEO-evidence fact-check, flag H1).
- **Data must be real.** A published index or benchmark with weak method would damage trust; use a method, sample size and date on every number, and mark gaps **TO CONFIRM with Quotr**.
