# Content Priorities: What Types of Content Quotr Should Build for GEO/AEO

> [!abstract] What this page is for
> The ranked list of content types Quotr.ai should put its time into so AI answer engines (ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews and AI Mode, Perplexity, Gemini, Claude, Copilot) name, cite and recommend Quotr. For each type: why it works (evidence), which buyer questions and funnel stage it wins, effort, expected impact, specific titles to build, and what to stop doing. It answers the meeting question **"What types of content should we prioritize for GEO/AEO?"**

## The short answer

**Put first the content an AI cannot piece together from anywhere else: content built on Quotr's own data, customers and people.** In priority order:

1. **Original data and benchmarks** (a factory-direct vs US price index, residential and multifamily cost-per-sq-ft data from Quotr Service jobs, an AI-takeoff accuracy benchmark) and the cost guides built on that data.
2. **Free tools:** trade material calculators, a landed-cost (DDP) calculator, and downloadable estimate, bid-leveling and pro forma templates.
3. **A few honest comparison pages** (5–8 head-to-heads, fact-checked, dated, saying where each rival is stronger), not more self-ranked "best of" lists.
4. **Video** on YouTube: one trade per video on real residential plans, plus customer jobs, with "Quotr.ai" said out loud and written in titles.
5. **Proof pages:** case studies with numbers and named customers, an accuracy method page, a security page, an integrations page, author pages.

Then, in support: neutral decision guides for the topics no software vendor owns yet (factory-direct buying, importing, tariffs, landed cost), 3–4 genuinely different persona hubs, deeper trade pages, refreshed how-tos, a small set of deepened glossary terms, and company news and press releases.

**Before any of it:** fix the facts on the pages AI already reads (old prices, factory count, HQ, turnaround) and write key sentences so the Quotr name travels with the fact. That work is in [[Optimize vs create]].

**The one rule for every new page:** *it must contain something no competitor or AI could write without Quotr's data, customers or people* (report, "Existing vs new content").

## Words used on this page

- **GEO / AEO:** generative engine optimization / answer engine optimization. Getting a brand named and linked inside AI answers.
- **Cited:** the AI used a page as a source (a footnote link). **Named:** the AI said "Quotr" in its answer. Quotr's problem today is that it is often cited but not named.
- **Unbranded question:** a question that does not mention Quotr, such as "best AI takeoff software for subcontractors 2026".
- **Funnel stages:** **TOFU** (top of funnel) = learning about a problem; **MOFU** (middle) = comparing tools, services or suppliers; **BOFU** (bottom) = checking Quotr itself (price, trust, fit).
- **Prompt IDs:** L-### (learn), E-### (evaluate/compare) and D-### (decide/brand) refer to rows in [[Prompt library]]. T## refers to the monthly tracking prompts in [[Tracking set]].
- **Click resilience:** whether a person still needs to visit the page after reading the AI summary (for example, to use a tool, download a file or check live numbers). Framework from Aleyda Solis ([content prioritization in an AI search era](https://www.aleydasolis.com/en/ai-search/content-prioritization-ai-search/)).
- **Takeoff:** measuring material quantities from building plans. **Landed cost / DDP:** the full delivered cost of imported goods; DDP (delivered duty paid) means the seller covers shipping, import duty and delivery.

## How we ranked the content types

Each type was scored on five questions. Scores are our judgment, based on the evidence above.

| Question | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| 1. **Quotr-only?** Can Quotr say something no rival or AI can? | Google's "non-commodity" test; Growth Memo's 3.3× finding |
| 2. **Open field?** Are software vendors absent from AI answers on this topic today? | White space is cheaper to win (see [[White space]]) |
| 3. **Click resilience?** Will people still click after reading the AI answer? | AI answers take clicks from generic content (see [[Top-of-funnel strategy]]) |
| 4. **Brand-mention potential?** Is there a natural reason for the AI to say "Quotr"? | Quotr's gap is being named, not being cited |
| 5. **Effort and dependency?** How much time, and does it need data or approvals from Quotr? | A seed-stage team must pick carefully |

**Effort scale** (our estimate): **S** = under one week for one person; **M** = 1–3 weeks; **L** = 1–2 months, or needs data work and approvals from Quotr.

**Impact scale** (our judgment): **High** = likely to change whether Quotr is named in tracked prompts or to create a new citable source; **Medium** = helps citations or conversion on a narrower set of prompts; **Low** = hygiene or long-term.

## 0. Foundation: fix facts and attach the brand (do first)

This is not a new content type, but nothing below works without it.

- **Why:** most errors in AI answers about Quotr trace back to Quotr's own pages. Perplexity quoted a starting price of "$299.90/month" (nearly four times the real $79.90 entry price) because about **13 Quotr URLs** and llms.txt still show retired "Solo/Team" pricing. It reported "50+ to 220+ factories, depending on the page" (report; verification file claims 7, 11).
- **Brand-attached facts:** AI will take a number and drop the brand. Rewrite key sentences so the name travels with the fact. Example from the report: *"Quotr.ai's Estimation Service charges $0.25 per sq ft for projects under 50,000 sq ft."* Start with the pages AI already fetches: [outsource-construction-estimating](https://quotr.ai/blog/outsource-construction-estimating/), [commercial-estimating-services](https://quotr.ai/blog/commercial-estimating-services/), [is-ai-takeoff-actually-accurate-yet](https://quotr.ai/blog/is-ai-takeoff-actually-accurate-yet/), [best-planswift-alternatives-2026](https://quotr.ai/blog/best-planswift-alternatives-2026/), [how-to-estimate-plumbing-from-drawings](https://quotr.ai/blog/how-to-estimate-plumbing-from-drawings/), [quantity-takeoff-services](https://quotr.ai/blog/quantity-takeoff-services/).
- **Effort:** S–M (fact sheet 1–2 days of leadership time; pricing sweep 1–2 days; brand-attach rewrite about 1 day).
- **Expected impact:** High and fast. If it works, branded answers should start quoting $79.90 once engines re-read the fixed pages (report, conclusion).
- **Full list and page-by-page actions:** [[Optimize vs create]]. Canonical facts: [[Entity fact sheet]].

## 1. Original data and benchmarks (Rank 1)

**Why (evidence)**
- Primary research earned 3.3× more AI citations per page in Growth Memo's study (see table above).
- Tariff answers cited **no software vendor**; multifamily cost answers cited small vendors (Meltplan, Exayard) next to RSMeans; the LA-fire-rebuild cost question cited Bloomberg and local contractors, not Quotr, even though Quotr sells a "Fast Cost Estimation (Residential LA Fire Rebuilding)" sample deliverable ([/service/](https://quotr.ai/service/); verification file, re-run table).
- Rivals already use data as proof: Togal.AI's peer-reviewed study with the University of Kansas (takeoffs "up to 76% faster" and "within 5%") ([Togal](https://www.togal.ai/case-study/peer-reviewed-study-togal-ai-vs-on-screen-takeoff)); Handoff's AI "trained on 68 million construction costs" ([Handoff](https://www.handoff.ai/blog/residential-contractor-guide-to-ai)).
- Quotr already has the raw material, but only as unsupported claims: "$1.2B+" of projects estimated and "300+ projects a month" ([blog index](https://quotr.ai/blog/)); real procurement prices such as the Saratoga (Myren Dr) job at $97,000 against a $187K–$218K Bay Area market price ([/procurement/](https://quotr.ai/procurement/)); "95–99% accuracy on clean vector PDFs (Quotr internal benchmarking)" ([Togal alternatives post](https://quotr.ai/blog/best-togal-ai-alternatives-2026/)). The report calls turning this into a dated dataset with a clear method "the biggest content opportunity in this audit".

**What it wins**
- TOFU cost and tariff questions: L-113 "how are 2026 tariffs affecting building material costs…" (T46), L-114 cabinet and vanity tariffs (T48), L-130/L-131 multifamily cost per sq ft and per unit (T45), L-132 house cost per sq ft in California, L-133 ADU cost, L-134 LA fire rebuild cost (T47), L-093/L-098/L-104 trade cost benchmarks.
- Trust and evaluation questions: L-035 "how accurate is AI takeoff" (T30), L-040 "how much time does AI takeoff actually save", L-006/L-007/L-046 "how long does an estimate take" (T32), D-019 "how accurate is Quotr's AI takeoff".
- Press and list editors: data is the hook for trade press and for the lists AI cites (see [[Off-site earned media plan]]).

**Effort:** L. Needs Quotr to share and approve data, a written method (sample size, date range, how prices were collected), and for tariff content an expert review. Every dataset is **TO CONFIRM with Quotr**: what can be published, in what aggregate, and with what customer permissions.

**Expected impact:** High. This is the only content type with both strong citation evidence and an open field.

**What Quotr should build (specific titles)**
| Working title | Data source inside Quotr | Cadence |
|---|---|---|
| "Factory-Direct vs US Dealer Price Index, Q4 2026: cabinets, vanities, tile, LVP flooring, interior doors, windows, plumbing fixtures" | Procurement quotes vs local dealer quotes (TO CONFIRM) | Quarterly |
| "What tariffs add to one house: a line-item example (2026)" | One real residential takeoff + current tariff rates (expert review needed) | When tariffs change |
| "Residential and Multifamily Construction Cost per Sq Ft by Trade, H2 2026 (from Quotr Service estimates)" | Aggregated Estimation Service jobs (TO CONFIRM) | Twice a year |
| "2026 AI Takeoff Accuracy Benchmark: residential and multifamily plan sets" | Internal benchmarking, published with method (vector vs scanned plans, items measured, error rates, time) | Yearly |
| "How long does a construction estimate take? Benchmarks by project size from Quotr Service jobs" | Service job timestamps (TO CONFIRM) | Twice a year |
| "Building material lead times, monthly: windows, cabinets, tile, flooring, fixtures" | Procurement orders (TO CONFIRM) | Monthly |
| "How Quotr counts its $1.2B+ in estimated projects: method and data" | Service records (TO CONFIRM) | Once, then yearly |

**Rules and watch-outs**
- Every number: dated, with method, sample size and source. Never publish an invented or estimated-looking number; mark gaps "TO CONFIRM with Quotr".
- Put the brand in the finding: "Quotr.ai's Q4 2026 index found…", not "our data shows…".
- Use a stable, year-free URL for recurring data (for example `/data/factory-direct-price-index/`) and add each edition inside it, so links and citations build up in one place.
- Offer a downloadable table (CSV or PDF) and a short chart. Pitch each edition (see [[Off-site earned media plan]]).
- Template: [[Original research report template]].

## 2. Free tools: calculators and downloadable templates (Rank 2)

**Why (evidence)**
- For "how to estimate drywall for a house" (P2), most sources were calculators, including free ones from rivals Easy Takeoffs, Procore and BuildVision ([Easy Takeoffs](https://easytakeoffs.com/calculators/drywall); [Procore](https://www.procore.com/library/calculators/drywall-calculator)).
- People still click through to use a tool after reading an AI summary (report; Aleyda Solis's "click resilience" dimension).
- Google AI Mode cites templates and integrations pages for SaaS (a qualitative finding from [Aleyda Solis](https://www.aleydasolis.com/en/ai-search/saas-ai-search-optimization/), reported in the playbook notes; not re-checked). Rivals already publish templates: Exayard ("estimating template free"), Struvia, Meltplan and Downtobid (bid-leveling spreadsheets; prompt L-015).
- Quotr has only one tool today, the [ROI calculator](https://quotr.ai/roi-calculator/) (website audit §10).
- Caveat: no dataset measures citation lift from calculators; the evidence is observed citations plus practitioner opinion (playbook notes §1 gaps).

**What it wins:** TOFU how-to and cost questions: L-047/L-048 drywall (T27), L-057 flooring waste, L-062 tile, L-069 concrete, L-055 framing, L-112/L-119 landed cost, L-015 bid-leveling template, L-138 pro forma. Tools also feed email capture (see [[Top-of-funnel strategy]]).

**Effort:** M per tool (spec, build, test, write the explainer around it). Templates: S–M.

**Expected impact:** High for citations on how-to prompts and for repeat visits; medium for brand naming (the tool page must say "Quotr.ai's free drywall calculator").

## 3. A few honest, maintained comparison pages (Rank 3)

**Why (evidence)**
- Comparison queries almost always trigger a Google AI Overview (95.4%, Seer).
- Quotr controls its comparison story today: in "Quotr.ai vs Togal.AI", about 10 of 20 sources were Quotr's own pages, and the framing was favourable (report; visibility notes §4). No competitor comparison page targeting Quotr was found (competitor notes §4).
- But the same pages spread errors: the stale "$299.90" price, "PlanSwift is a Trimble product" (it belongs to ConstructConnect), and leftover internal notes such as "Quotr.ai should win when the buyer is asking…" (Quotr fact-check, summary item 10; report).
- Self-ranking #1 is risky (Lily Ray findings above) and Perplexity does not accept it (V3).

**What it wins:** BOFU brand comparisons (D-021/D-022 Quotr vs Togal, T17 and T37; D-024/D-025 four-way; D-026 vs STACK; D-027 vs PlanSwift; D-029 vs Handoff; D-030 vs Buildxact; D-031 vs Kreo; D-023 "Quotr alternatives", T40) and some MOFU alternatives prompts (E-043 Togal alternatives, T16; E-044 PlanSwift alternatives, T18). Unbranded "alternatives" answers are mostly won off-site (G2/Capterra alternatives pages).

**Effort:** S per fix; M per new page. Ongoing: re-check prices and facts quarterly or whenever a rival changes pricing.

**Expected impact:** Medium–High for brand answers (accuracy of what AI says about Quotr vs rivals); low for unbranded answers unless third-party proof exists.

## 4. Video (Rank 4)

**Why (evidence)**
- Aleyda Solis's August 2026 study found YouTube to be the **largest single source site** for SaaS brands in AI answers ([Aleyda Solis](https://www.aleydasolis.com/en/ai-search/ai-search-citations/)).
- Ahrefs found YouTube mentions are the strongest **correlate** of AI visibility across 75,000 brands (a link in the data, not proof of cause) ([Ahrefs](https://ahrefs.com/blog/ai-brand-visibility-correlations/)).
- A separate Aleyda Solis SaaS analysis (reported in the playbook notes §1; not re-checked by the fact-check) put video at 23.0% of cited sources in Google AI Mode but only 1.0% in ChatGPT, and Perplexity **never cited YouTube** in our 45 runs. So the payoff is most likely in Google's AI features, which we could not test.
- Buyers watch demos: r/estimators users ask whether AI takeoff really works, and competitors (Togal webinars, Beam's BuildUp Podcast, Buildxact tutorials) already use video (citation map §3g).

**What it wins:** TOFU how-to prompts where a walkthrough helps (L-051 drywall takeoff, L-059 flooring takeoff, L-091 electrical, L-097 plumbing, L-102 HVAC, L-031 takeoff from PDF), trust prompts (L-041 "are there any AI takeoff tools that work", D-019), and procurement proof (L-121 quality checks).

**Effort:** M, ongoing (about 2 videos a month is a realistic start; capacity **TO CONFIRM with Quotr**).

**Expected impact:** Medium, rising over time; also builds brand search and trust with human buyers.

**What Quotr should build**

## Quality bar for every new or rebuilt page

A page ships only if it passes all of these. Full style rules: [[GEO writing style guide]].

1. **Quotr-only test:** it contains something no competitor or AI could write without Quotr's data, customers or people.
2. **Facts match the fact sheet** ([[Entity fact sheet]]); nothing marked TO CONFIRM is published as fact.
3. **Answer first:** the direct answer in the first one or two sentences (Quotr already does this well; keep it).
4. **Brand-attached key facts:** "Quotr.ai's …" in the sentences AI is most likely to lift.
5. **Every number dated and sourced;** Quotr's own numbers have a method.
6. **Named human author** with a bio page; an expert reviewer for technical, tariff or certification topics.
7. **Honest limits** (for example: accuracy drops on scanned plans; local supply wins for urgent items).
8. **No self-ranking, no bot-directed wording, no year bump without real changes.**
9. **Distinct from every other Quotr page** (no near-duplicates; check the page-by-page table first).
10. **Linked into a hub** and to the relevant tool, dataset or product page; basic schema per [[Schema markup kit]].
11. **Tracked:** the target prompts are in [[Tracking set]] or added to the prompt library.
