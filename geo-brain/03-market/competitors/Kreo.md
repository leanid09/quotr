## Overview

Kreo is the tool AI engines pick when someone asks for "cheap" or "budget" AI takeoff. Listicles quote it at "about $35 per user per month", and Perplexity repeated that in V5 ("Kreo vs Togal") and V7 ("cheapest AI takeoff software").

**The fine print matters.** Kreo's own pricing page describes Lite ($35) as "for teams to collaborate on drawings **without AI-powered tools**" and Plus ($70) as "ideal for managing project measurements **without AI**". The core AI takeoff tools (Auto Measure, One-Click Area, Auto Count) are in **Pro at $175** per user per month. So "AI takeoff from $35" is not quite what Kreo sells.

## Strengths

- **Price anchor.** Every "cheapest" list starts with Kreo.
- **Fast, frequent product news.** Recent posts: "AI Classification, PDF Bookmarks, and More Reliable Takeoff" (Sep 17, 2026), "Auto Measure 3.0: the best AI model in the world for quantity takeoff" (Aug 28, 2026), "Caddie Saves Your Chats…" (Aug 25, 2026) ([Kreo blog](https://www.kreo.net/news-2d-takeoff)).
- **Self-serve with a free trial** and a money-back guarantee.
- **International** (three currencies; UK enterprise customers).
- **Reviews on both G2 and Capterra**, though small.

## Weaknesses

- **"Cheap" and "AI" live in different plans**, which a careful comparison can point out.
- **Small review base** (32–39 G2, 25 Capterra).
- **Name collision:** a Trustpilot page for "Kreo Tech" (kreo-tech.com) rates 2.1/5 and appears to be a different company ([Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/kreo-tech.com); not verified).
- **No estimating service, no procurement.**
- **Reviewers mention slowness with large files** (G2 search summary).

## What Kreo says about Quotr

Nothing found (WebSearch for Quotr on kreo.net, 2026-09-25).

**But the "Kreo alternatives" space already names Quotr.** Nomic's "Best Kreo Alternatives in 2026" (last reviewed September 2026) lists Quotr.ai #5 of 7 as "AI takeoff-to-proposal automation for contractors… Pricing: From ~$299.90/month". ## How Quotr can win against Kreo in AI answers

**Strategy: win "cheapest AI takeoff" on honest, feature-level price comparison, and build on the one list that already names Quotr.**

1. **Publish "Quotr.ai vs Kreo" and a "Kreo alternatives" page.** Neither exists. The query already has a third-party list that includes Quotr, so it is a realistic opening.
2. **Make the price comparison feature-based and dated.** A table of "cheapest plan that includes AI auto-measure/auto-count" for Kreo, Togal, STACK, Quotr, Easy Takeoffs and QuoteIQ, with a link to each vendor's pricing page and the date checked. Kreo's AI tools start at $175 (Pro); Quotr Lite lists AI detection at $79.90 (TO CONFIRM). Fair, sourced tables get used as fact sources even when self-ranking is ignored.
3. **Ask Nomic to update Quotr's entry.** Send the current price (Lite $79.90 / Plus $299.90), the procurement and Estimation Service facts, and a demo login. This is the cheapest third-party fix available. See [../citation-sources-map.md](../citation-sources-map.md).
4. **Get into the "cheapest" and "free trial" answers.** V7 and V8 were built from Easy Takeoffs, Pilars, ScopeTakeoff, TDPM, ContraVault, Dan Cumberland Labs and vendor pricing pages. Quotr's 7-day free trial and $79.90 Lite were absent. Add "7-day free trial, cancel anytime, no charge if canceled" in plain text near the top of pricing and comparison pages, and pitch TDPM, which was cited in both prompts.
5. **Answer the "is cheap AI takeoff accurate?" follow-up.** Pair price pages with Quotr's accuracy explainer ([is-ai-takeoff-actually-accurate-yet](https://quotr.ai/blog/is-ai-takeoff-actually-accurate-yet/)), made brand-attributed and method-backed.
6. **Target these prompts:** "cheapest AI takeoff software", "AI takeoff software with free trial", "Kreo alternatives", "Kreo vs Quotr", "Kreo vs Togal vs Quotr", "affordable AI takeoff for small contractors".
