## Words used on this page

- **AI answer engine:** a tool that writes an answer instead of showing ten links (ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews / AI Mode, Perplexity, Gemini, Claude, Copilot).
- **Named / recommended:** the brand appears in the answer text.
- **Cited:** a page is used as a source link in the answer.
- **Share of voice (SOV):** a brand's share of all brand mentions across a set of AI answers.
- **Unbranded prompt:** a question that does not name any vendor (for example "best AI takeoff software 2026").
- **Takeoff:** measuring and counting quantities (areas, lengths, fixtures) from construction drawings.
- **Estimating:** turning quantities into a priced bid (materials + labor + markup).
- **Bid leveling:** comparing several subcontractor or supplier bids side by side on the same scope.
- **Procurement / buyout:** actually buying the materials after the job is won.
- **Listicle:** a "Best 10 X in 2026" style article.

**Important limit:** all live AI tests behind this page were run on **Perplexity (Sonar model) only**, on 2026-09-25, mostly once per prompt. ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews/AI Mode, Gemini, Claude and Copilot were **not** tested. Treat the share-of-voice numbers as a directional snapshot, not a fixed ranking. See [[AI visibility baseline]] for the full method.

## The short version

1. **No single vendor owns this category.** Different vendors win different kinds of questions.
2. **Head-term "best AI takeoff" answers** go to **Togal.AI, STACK, PlanSwift and Beam AI** (prompt C1, reproduced on re-run).
3. **"Best AI estimating" and residential answers** go to **Handoff, Buildxact and Togal.AI**, with Houzz Pro, Buildertrend and Clear Estimates for classic residential estimating (C2, C11, C13).
4. **Multifamily answers** go to **Autodesk (Takeoff / Forma Estimate)**, plus STACK, PlanSwift, RSMeans and Trimble (C8 and the multifamily benchmark prompt).
5. **Bid-management and bid-leveling answers** go to **ConstructConnect, BuildingConnected, SmartBid, PlanHub, Procore, Buildertrend and Buildxact** (C7 and the bid-leveling benchmark prompt).
6. **"Procurement software" answers** go to **Procore, Buildertrend, Buildxact, Archdesk, Trimble Materials and Field Materials AI**. **Factory-direct buying** answers go to **sourcing companies** such as Alibaba, Port2Site and Build Source, not to software (C9, C10, C14).
7. **Quotr.ai was named in 1 of 32 unbranded prompts (about 3%), while STACK, PlanSwift and Buildxact were each named in 10.** Counted as a share of all brand mentions, Quotr's share of voice is about 0.7%. Quotr appears mainly when the question names Quotr, names a competitor Quotr has written about, or closely copies the wording of a Quotr blog post.
8. **Why the winners win:** large review counts on G2 and Capterra, a place on the G2/Capterra "alternatives" pages, inclusion in independent "best of 2026" lists, their own dated listicles and "vs" page hubs, trade pages and free calculators, and a clear persona ("for remodelers", "for multifamily").

### Why these brands win (the seven patterns)

1. **Review volume.** Third-party list writers build their tables from G2 and Capterra numbers. ConstructConnect's guide lists a G2 rating for every product it covers. A product with 0 reviews has nothing for them to quote. Quotr's G2 profile appears to have 0 reviews (reported by Perplexity; G2 blocks direct checks).
2. **A spot on the G2/Capterra "alternatives" pages.** In "X alternatives" questions, Perplexity takes its lead picks from these pages (for example Groundplan as "Capterra's best overall" for PlanSwift alternatives). Quotr is not on them.
3. **Inclusion in independent-looking "best of 2026" lists.** Construction Coverage, The Digital Project Manager (TDPM), ConstructionPlacements, Dan Cumberland Labs and the ConstructConnect guide are cited again and again. Quotr is not in any of them (site-restricted search check, 2026-09-25).
4. **Their own dated listicles and "vs" hubs.** Handoff ranks itself first in its own "6 best AI estimating software 2026" post, and that post was the first citation in the residential test. Beam AI runs 20+ comparison pages. Easy Takeoffs and BuildVision publish one "alternatives" post per incumbent. Quotr uses the same tactic, and it works for Quotr only when the question names a competitor.
5. **Trade pages and free tools.** Beam, STACK, Kreo and Togal have rich trade pages. BuildVision, Easy Takeoffs and Procore win "how to estimate drywall" citations with free calculators. Quotr has 23 trade pages, but they are thin (about 25 unique words on the drywall page), and it has no trade calculators.
6. **A clear persona.** Handoff says "for remodelers and home builders" everywhere. Autodesk is the multifamily default. Buildxact is "small residential builders". Quotr's pages say residential in some places and commercial in others.
7. **Citable data.** Togal has a peer-reviewed study. Handoff claims AI "trained on 68 million construction costs". Meltplan and Exayard get cited next to RSMeans for cost-per-square-foot numbers. Quotr's strongest numbers (95–99% accuracy, $1.2B+ estimated) are self-published without method, so AI engines hedge or anonymize them.
