## What it covers

A step-by-step guide for subcontractors: review the invitation to bid, download plans, specs and addenda, confirm scope, do the takeoff, price labor and materials, add overhead and profit, write the proposal, submit on time and follow up. It explains estimate vs bid vs proposal, lists what a sub bid should include (inclusions, exclusions, alternates, addenda, unit prices, allowances, expiry date), and names Quotr.ai as a tool for bid packages and faster takeoff. *(from web search results)*
