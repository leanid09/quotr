# Q-53 · Blog schedule and AI tools

> [!question] Question for Quotr
> **Blog schedule and AI tools:** which AI tools help draft the posts? Why do most posts go out on Tuesdays and Thursdays, often 2 or 3 on the same day (a set schedule, or a queue of finished drafts)? Why did output fall from 25 posts in June to 7 so far in September? Who will update older posts when a fact such as a price changes?

**Why we ask:** to set a pace and an edit step that fit how the team already works. See [[Publishing patterns and correlations]] and [[Blog health audit]].

- **Weekdays.** 56 of the 90 posts with real dates (62%) went out on a Tuesday or Thursday. None went out at a weekend.
- **Same-day batches.** 16 days had 2 or more posts, 35 posts in all (39%). Three days had 3 posts: 2026-05-07, 2026-06-02 and 2026-07-07.
- **Pace.** About 5.4 posts a week from mid-May to mid-July, and about 2.3 a week from August. The slower pace is fine. It is close to the 8 to 12 pieces a month the plan recommends.
- **AI tools.** In 2 of about 6 posts read in full, notes from the writing brief or ChatGPT tracking tags (utm_source=chatgpt.com) reached the live page ([[What went wrong#W-12 Output grew faster than the editing check|W-12]]). Knowing the tools helps us design the edit step ([[A16 Add a human edit and fact-check step for AI-assisted drafts|A16]]).
- **Updates to older posts.** 11 days after the 2026-09-14 price change, 13 to 16 posts still seemed to show retired prices ([[What went wrong#W-01 Old prices still showing after the September price change|W-01]]). We do not know who owns these updates.
- **Already asked.** [[Q-42 Process and plans|Q-42]] asks how content is made and who edits AI drafts. [[Q-41 Owners and budget|Q-41]] asks about owners and budget. This question asks about tools, timing and updates.
