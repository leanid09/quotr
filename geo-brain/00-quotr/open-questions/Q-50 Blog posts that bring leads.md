# Q-50 · Blog posts that bring leads

> [!question] Question for Quotr
> **Blog posts that bring leads:** can Quotr see which blog posts lead to a trial, a demo booking or a Service quote request? Is GA4 on both quotr.ai and the app sign-up flow, so a visit can be followed from a post to a sign-up? Which posts does the sales team send to prospects, and do buyers ever mention a post?

**Why we ask:** we want to refresh the posts that bring business first, and to show Quotr what the work is worth. See [[Blog health audit]] and [[Retainer scope and value case]].

- **What we have today.** We judge posts only by AI tests and web search checks. In the Perplexity tests of 2026-09-25, 12 of the 96 posts were flagged as sources. A re-check of the test notes puts the real number at 10 ([[What went wrong#W-04 AI uses Quotr's figures but drops the Quotr name|W-04]]). We have no visits or leads per post.
- **GA4 on the sign-up flow.** GA4 (Google Analytics 4, the site's visitor statistics) can link a post to a sign-up only if it also runs on the app's sign-up pages. [[Tracking setup]] §9 lists this as TO CONFIRM.
- **Sales stories count too.** A buyer who says "I read your STACK comparison" is evidence that no tool shows.
- **Already asked.** [[Q-40 Access and history|Q-40]] asks for GA4 and CRM access, and which key events (the actions GA4 counts as conversions) exist. This question asks whether leads can be traced back to single posts.
