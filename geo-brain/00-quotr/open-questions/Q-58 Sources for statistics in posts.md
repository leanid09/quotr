# Q-58 · Sources for statistics in posts

> [!question] Question for Quotr
> **Sources for statistics in posts:** where do these numbers come from? The 12.6% cost rise in the URL of the "construction costs surged" post, and four figures in the scope-gap post: "$177 billion a year", "8-14% of contract value" for change orders, "80% ... trace to missing or poor information" and "around 5%" rework.

**Why we ask:** these numbers have no link, date or method, and we could not trace them. If the writers have sources, we add them. If not, we remove or relabel the numbers. See [[What went wrong#W-13 Statistics and competitor facts went out without sources|W-13]] and [[Blog health audit]].

- **Scope-gap post.** [[BP-92 scope gap construction|BP-92]] carries the CTO's byline (Junzhe Shi, PhD). A wrong number there hurts Quotr's strongest trust asset.
- **What we found.** Search found no match for "8-14%". The $177 billion figure may come from a study of wasted labour, not scope gaps (inference).
- **12.6% post.** For [[BP-70 construction costs surged 12 6 in 2026 how ai estimation helps|BP-70]], AGC reports input costs up 8.9% from August 2025 to August 2026, which does not match 12.6%. If no source exists, the plan is to merge BP-70 into [[BP-03 construction cost trends 2026|BP-03]] with a 301 redirect.
- **Fix and prevention.** Sources go in during [[A6 Editorial sweep|A6]]. From now on, every number needs a source and a date before a post goes live ([[A16 Add a human edit and fact-check step for AI-assisted drafts|A16]]).
