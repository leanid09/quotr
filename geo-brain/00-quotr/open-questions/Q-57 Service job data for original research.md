# Q-57 · Service job data for original research

> [!question] Question for Quotr
> **Service job data for original research:** can Quotr share anonymised Estimation Service job data, for example cost per sq ft by trade and by project type (residential, multifamily, commercial)? How many jobs and what date range are available? Who can approve publishing it, and which details must stay private?

**Why we ask:** numbers that only Quotr has give AI engines a reason to name Quotr, and the blog has almost none. See [[What went wrong#W-05 Little original data, and headline numbers have no method|W-05]] and [[Blog health audit]].

- **Borrowed numbers.** The 6 cost and market posts and the "State of AI in Preconstruction" report summarise other people's numbers.
- **Service posts.** 11 of the 18 posts in August and September were about the Service line. We found none that publishes data from Service jobs (only about 6 posts were read in full).
- **Name dropped.** In one test (C12), Perplexity used Quotr's Service rates ($0.25 and $0.10 per sq ft) but credited them to "one outsourced estimating service" ([[What went wrong#W-04 AI uses Quotr's figures but drops the Quotr name|W-04]]).
- **Planned report.** Task [[D2 Residential and multifamily cost per sq ft by trade (R-40)|D2]] (report [[R-40 Residential and Multifamily Construction Cost per Sq Ft by Trade|R-40]], planned for January to March 2027) needs a Service data export. No open question asks for it.
- **Already asked.** [[Q-14 Data|Q-14]] covers procurement price data. [[Q-17 Volume claims|Q-17]] covers how "$1.2B+" and "300+ projects a month" are counted. [[Q-18 Accuracy|Q-18]] covers the accuracy benchmark.
