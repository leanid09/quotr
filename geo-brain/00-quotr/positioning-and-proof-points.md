## Key terms

- **Positioning** = the short answer to "what is this, who is it for, and why pick it over the alternatives?"
- **Proof point** = a fact a stranger can check that supports a claim (a named customer result, a review score, a third-party article, published data).
- **"Index-only"** = seen only in a search engine's stored copy of a quotr.ai page (checked with WebSearch on 2026-09-25), not on the live page. It may be out of date.
- **Why this matters for GEO:** AI answer engines repeat claims they can check against several sources. In the tests, Perplexity described Quotr accurately on branded questions but added hedges like "vendor assertions", "not independently well-validated" and "many of the comparison claims come from Quotr.ai's own blog content" (visibility tests §4; verification file, B4 re-run). Positioning only turns into recommendations when third parties back it up.

## 2. Differentiators

### 2.1 The unique combination: takeoff + estimating + bids + factory-direct procurement

**Claim:** Quotr.ai is the only AI takeoff and estimating platform found in the research that also sells factory-direct materials delivered duty-paid to the jobsite.

**Evidence:**
- Quotr sells all three lines: Quotr Software, Quotr Service, Quotr Procurement ([homepage](https://quotr.ai/)).
- The lines are linked in the product: the software has in-app quote requests and side-by-side bid comparison, and the Plus plan includes "10% off procurement" and takeoff credits ([/software/](https://quotr.ai/software/), scraped 2026-09-25). The page's own line: "One platform from takeoff to purchase order".
- The closest competitor bundles are domestic, not factory-direct:
  - **Handoff** offers "materials purchasing through Lowe's" alongside estimating: its AI matches estimate items to the Lowe's catalog (about 100,000 materials) with live local pricing ([Handoff listicle](https://www.handoff.ai/blog/6-best-ai-construction-estimating-software-2026-picks-compared); [Handoff Lowe's integration post](https://www.handoff.ai/blog/product-update-lowe-s-integration); search summaries, WebSearch 2026-09-25). That is domestic retail, not factory-direct.
  - **Buildxact** combines takeoffs with live pricing from local dealers and a Home Depot integration that turns estimates into orderable material lists ([Buildxact estimating](https://www.buildxact.com/us/features/construction-estimating-software/); [Buildxact dealer connection](https://www.buildxact.com/us/features/builder-remodeler/dealer-connection/); search summaries, WebSearch 2026-09-25). Also domestic retail and dealers, not factory-direct.
  - Factory-direct sourcing players surfaced by AI (FBM Sourcing, Go Global, Trillion Sources, Spotyard) have no AI takeoff or estimating software (competitor benchmark §1).
- Perplexity, asked for factory-direct procurement platforms, said: "the search results do not show a single platform explicitly marketed as a factory-direct marketplace for all building materials" (offsite notes §2).
- A WebSearch on 2026-09-25 for "AI takeoff estimating software with factory-direct building materials procurement platform 2026" named Quotr.ai as the key platform, but the summary came from Quotr's own roundup post ("The Procurement Wedge … 220+ vetted factories … 40–50% below retail markup"), so it repeated the conflicting factory and savings numbers ([Quotr roundup](https://quotr.ai/blog/best-ai-construction-estimating-software-2026/)).

**Strength:** Strong as a fact; weak as a *recognised* position, because no third party says it yet.

### 2.2 Three ways to buy: do it yourself, done for you, and buy the materials

- **Do it yourself:** Quotr Software (from $79.90 per seat per month).
- **Done for you:** Quotr Service ($0.25/sq ft under 50,000 sq ft; $0.10/sq ft above).
- **Buy the materials:** Quotr Procurement (DDP pricing).
- Quotr's own homepage wording for this: three "lanes" — "hands-off estimating support, in-house software, or factory-direct procurement savings" (search-index text, WebSearch 2026-09-25). Reusing this exact phrase across the site, profiles and boilerplate would help AI tools repeat it consistently.
- The service and software are joined up: Quotr says "every Quotr Service engagement includes the software", so the customer gets an editable project with sheet-level references, not a static spreadsheet (index-only text of the [electrical estimating services post](https://quotr.ai/blog/electrical-estimating-services/); **TO CONFIRM**). This is a strong, concrete point versus traditional estimating firms.
- Nearest competitor pattern: Beam AI sells AI plus human-reviewed takeoffs in 24–48 hours ([PR Newswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/beam-ai-to-showcase-instant-ai-hvac-takeoffs-at-ahr-expo-2026-302675290.html)), which mirrors Quotr Service, but no Beam procurement offer was seen in the research.

### 2.4 An AI Agent that answers questions about the drawings

- "Ask in plain language — the AI Agent reads your plans directly. Get symbol counts, room dimensions, and spec answers without hunting through the drawings" (live [/software/](https://quotr.ai/software/), scraped 2026-09-25). Lite has a "Basic AI agent"; Plus has "Advanced AI".
- Balanced framing in Quotr's own post: "the estimator still owns the final number" (index-only text of the [chat-with-blueprints post](https://quotr.ai/blog/ai-that-reads-construction-drawings-chat-with-blueprints/)).
- "Smart Matching" gives each counted item a confidence score and an audit trail back to the drawing, so the estimator knows what to double-check (index-only text of Quotr pages; **TO CONFIRM** feature name). This speaks directly to buyers' #1 worry about AI: accuracy.
- Many competitors now claim AI features (for example, STACK has announced "STACK IQ", conversational AI for estimating and preconstruction: [STACK blog](https://www.stackct.com/blog/stack-introduces-stack-iq-bringing-conversational-ai-to-estimating-and-preconstruction/)), so this is a supporting point, not a headline.

### 2.5 Browser-based, works on Mac and Windows

- Quotr's posts say it is "cloud-native", runs "in any modern browser on any operating system" and needs "no desktop install" (index-only text of the [PlanSwift comparison](https://quotr.ai/blog/quotr-ai-vs-planswift-ai-takeoff-procurement-comparison-2026/)).
- This matters against desktop incumbents (PlanSwift runs on Windows) and for the "takeoff software for Mac users" question (test C15), where AI tools named STACK, Easy Takeoffs, Square Takeoff, Bluebeam and Buildxact, but not Quotr.
- Many AI-native rivals are also browser-based, so use it as a supporting point.

### 2.6 Founders who come from design and engineering

- Hanyang Liu (CEO): studied architecture and spent "years at design firms watching projects get derailed the moment a great design met a cost estimate" (live [/about-us/](https://quotr.ai/about-us/), scraped 2026-09-25). Older search-index copies and LinkedIn name TAO and SmithGroup and a UC Berkeley architecture degree (**TO CONFIRM** before use).
- Junzhe Shi (CTO): "AI and systems engineering, building algorithms at Apple and UC Berkeley" (live /about-us/); PhD, UC Berkeley ([ResearchGate](https://www.researchgate.net/profile/Junzhe-Shi-2)).
- The two have "known each other since middle school" (live /about-us/).
- The founder story is a real "why": the design-to-cost gap. It is a strong, human angle for PR and for "About" pages.
- Backed by Berkeley SkyDeck (Batch 19) and Llama Ventures.
- This is credible expertise, but it is barely used: only 4 of the 12 newest posts carry Junzhe Shi's byline, and there are no author pages (onsite audit §3).

### 2.7 Breadth across trades

- 23 trade workflow pages; the service covers "26 sub-trades".
- Strongest depth: electrical, HVAC, plumbing, drywall, flooring and concrete (onsite audit §6).
- Weakness: at audit time 13+ trade pages were thin (the drywall page had about 25 words of unique copy) and had no supporting blog content, so the breadth claim is not yet backed by content. Some pages may have been expanded since; re-check.

### 2.8 Residential, multifamily and developer focus (potential, not yet owned)

- Quotr's own copy says residential single-family and multifamily is core (llms.txt, /faq/), and its procurement proof comes from three Bay Area residential addresses.
- AI answers for residential and multifamily questions currently go to Handoff, Buildxact, Autodesk and cost-benchmark pages (visibility tests C2, C8, C13).
- **Decision needed from Quotr:** which audience leads (residential/multifamily vs commercial). The site currently says both (see [entity-fact-sheet.md](entity-fact-sheet.md#4-inconsistency-register), row 18).

### 7.3 Messaging rules (recommendations)

**Do:**
- Lead with the workflow (plans → takeoff → estimate → bid → materials) and the three product lines.
- Use real numbers with their source ("RL Electric cut…", "on the Saratoga project…").
- Say "Quotr.ai" plus "construction takeoff and estimating" in the first sentence, so AI tools do not confuse it with namesakes.
- Admit limits (for example: accuracy drops on low-resolution scans; the estimator owns the final number). Balanced pages are trusted more.

**Don't:**
- Call procurement "not software" in a way that makes the whole company sound like a sourcing agent.
- Repeat unproven numbers from Section 6.
- Use retired plan names or prices.
- Write instructions aimed at AI systems or "algorithmic scrapers".
