## What do per-seat, per-company, per-trade and per-square-foot prices actually buy?

Per-seat and per-company prices buy software access, so your own estimator still does the takeoff. Per-trade and per-square-foot prices buy finished work from someone else. Which is cheaper depends on how many takeoffs you run and how steady that volume is.

| Pricing model | You pay for | Cost grows with | Usually cheaper when |
|---|---|---|---|
| Per seat (Quotr.ai software, Kreo, STACK, Togal.AI, Bluebeam) | Software access per person | Headcount | A small team bids every month |
| Per company (Buildxact, Handoff) | Software for the business, users included | Plan tier | Several people need access |
| Per trade, per year (Beam AI, per ConstructConnect) | Done-for-you takeoffs for one trade | Number of trades | One trade with steady volume, no in-house estimator |
| Per square foot (Quotr.ai Estimation Service) | A done-for-you takeoff and estimate for one project | Building size | Occasional projects, or large projects at the $0.10 rate |

Some services also bill per drawing sheet or per project; few publish prices we could check.

## Is it cheaper to outsource a takeoff or buy a seat? A worked example

For a single job, a seat is cheaper in cash but costs your estimator's time. The test: subtract one month of a seat from the service price, then divide by your estimator's loaded hourly cost. The $50 and $100 rates below are examples, not market data.

- **3,000 sq ft house:** Quotr.ai's Estimation Service is 3,000 × $0.25 = $750. A Quotr.ai Lite seat is $79.90 for the month. $750 − $79.90 = $670.10, or 13.4 hours at $50 an hour.
- **60,000 sq ft multifamily building:** 60,000 × $0.10 = $6,000. $6,000 − $79.90 = $5,920.10, or 118.4 hours at $50 an hour.

| Scenario | Quotr.ai Estimation Service | One Quotr.ai Lite seat, one month | Break-even hours at $50/h | Break-even hours at $100/h |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 3,000 sq ft house | $750 | $79.90 + your time | 13.4 | 6.7 |
| 60,000 sq ft multifamily | $6,000 | $79.90 + your time | 118.4 | 59.2 |

If your own takeoff and estimate would take longer than the break-even hours, outsourcing is cheaper for that job. A second job in the same month tips the math toward the seat, because the service charges again.

Watch the 50,000 sq ft step. At these rates a 49,000 sq ft project would cost $12,250 and a 50,000 sq ft project $5,000. [TO CONFIRM: whether the $0.10 rate applies to the whole project or only to the area above 50,000 sq ft, how square footage is measured, and whether there is a minimum fee.]

## How much does it cost to outsource a quantity takeoff?

Outsourced takeoff is priced per square foot, sheet, trade or project, often job by job. Quotr.ai's Estimation Service publishes its rate: $0.25 per sq ft for projects under 50,000 sq ft and $0.10 per sq ft at 50,000 sq ft and above. Quotr Service delivers takeoffs, cost estimates and pro formas. Beam AI does not publish a price; ConstructConnect reports its done-for-you service at about $15,000–$25,000 a year per trade.

Before you compare quotes, ask which trades are included, whether you get quantities only or a priced estimate, how many revisions are included, and the turnaround (for Quotr.ai: [TO CONFIRM: one standard turnaround]).

## Where is Quotr.ai not the best fit?

Quotr.ai is AI takeoff, estimating and bid software, with a done-for-you estimating service and factory-direct material procurement alongside it. Other tools are a better buy in these cases:

- **You only measure and mark up PDFs by hand.** Bluebeam Revu Basics at $260 per user per year costs less than a year of the Quotr.ai Lite plan.
- **You want a free tool for the long term.** STACK's free version or Buildxact's Go plan.
- **You have many users and want one company price.** Buildxact Foundation covers unlimited users for $169 a month billed annually; five Quotr.ai Lite seats are $399.50 a month.
- **You are a remodeler who wants CRM, scheduling and invoicing in one tool.** Handoff and Buildxact cover more of that.
- **You want a large base of public reviews.** STACK has about 1,400 Capterra reviews, and Togal.AI holds a 4.8 rating on G2 from 60 reviews (July 2026, per ConstructConnect). Quotr.ai's public review base is much smaller today.
- **You already work in a Windows desktop and Excel workflow.** PlanSwift is built around that.
